# The Live panel

The list to the strip's headline: what the house can still join or read, on
the right sidebar and in Zen's Live tile. The strip, its queue and its sources
are in `CONTEXT.md`.

The right sidebar's Live panel (`panel.rs`, `RightSidebarComponent::Live`, on by default, reordered and toggled like every panel) is the list to the strip's headline: four rows under a `── live ──` rule, one per thing the house can still join or read, for as long as that is true, not only while it is news. Four kinds: a stream that has gone live (a pending one has no stamp and is not listed), a live game on a watchable door, a timed event on the board from an hour before its start until it ends (kind `event`, soonest first, the start as a clock time in the viewer's zone, with `·N` in; an all-day event takes the #lounge topic row instead, and the viewer's own events never leave screen 7), and a News share younger than `NEWS_LIFETIME` (1h), still in view after its card in #lounge has scrolled away. Newest on top within a kind: what just started is what the room is talking about, and the hub rail keeps the door-by-door order for browsing. **The floor rule** (`panel::arrange`) shares the four rows: every kind with something gets one row first, then the leftover goes streams, then games, then events, then news, and what does not fit folds into `+N more` in the last row; so two streams never push the games off, and a stream and a game never push the fresh link off. Four of everything reads stream, game, link, `+9 more`; nothing live reads four links. The rows that made it show grouped, streams on top, games, events, news last, newest first within a kind: the floor rule decides what is on the panel, not where. A row is a seven-column kind, a middle, an edge, and its key at the right (`s1` to `s4`, in the music panel's `v1` key style). The kind is `stream` (amber), the game's name (`dcss`, `nethack`, `brogue`, `SpectateGame::slug`, dim), `event` (dim amber) or `news` (faint): what the row is, told apart at a glance by the word and its color. The middle is the streamer and the stream's title, where the game's player is as the host read it (or `12m in` until it has; the handle is the watch header's, not the row's), the event's start time and title, or the link's title. The edge: `·N` viewers for a stream (the watch page's count) or a game (the people with the watch open, `OpenWatches::watchers_of`), `·N` in for an event, an amber `●` for a link while it is unread (`news::state::Reads`, the badge's rule: no `article_reads` row of the reader's, so a visit to the News room clears every dot and opening the article clears its own; not how long ago, the panel's hour is short enough). None reads `nobody playing`; the panel never changes shape. `s` then the row's digit (`input::open_from_prefix`; `s` is the Live prefix, armed in `app/input.rs` on Home like `z`, except while RSS, Discover or News is selected, which spend `s` themselves, and in `zen/input.rs` while a Live tile is on the page, swallowing any other suffix) or a click on the row (`input::open_from_panel_click`) opens it exactly as the strip's click would, both over the targets (`panel::PanelTarget`) the last draw recorded in `LiveState::panel_hit`, so the key opens what the eye sees and does nothing while the rail is not drawn: the stream's room, the watch, the event's day, the article. A deadchannel run or fight joins as a `LivePanelRow` variant with its own text and its own `PanelTarget`.

## Zen's Live tile

Zen's Live tile (`TileKind::Live`, placed like any other) is not the strip: it draws the Live panel's rows ( `panel::draw_live_inline` over the same rows the frame built for the sidebar), the same four rows with their `s1` to `s4` keys, wider, so less is cut. The strip's sources that the panel does not list (a daily match, a booth track) are still on Zen through the status line's Live segment.
