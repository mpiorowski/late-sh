# Lateania: input and UI

The keys, panels and layouts of the active world. Part of the Lateania door: the screen lifecycle and the input-capture contract are `CONTEXT.md` §3, and the map pages are `WORLDMAP.md`. The `?` guide (`help_modal/data.rs::lateania_help_lines`) is the player-facing key reference; a key change updates both.

### Class selection

Before class choice:
- `w`/`s` (or `k`/`j`) move the highlight, Enter chooses it, and `1-9` quick-pick the first nine classes in `Class::ALL` order.
- `r`: reroll 4d6-drop-lowest ability scores.
- Other ordinary game keys are ignored.

While an attribute point waits to be placed (`PlayerView::score_offer` non-empty, which the view keeps empty while the level-10 archetype crossroads is open):
- `1-6`: place the point on STR/DEX/CON/INT/WIS/CHA (`Score::ALL` order). Every other ordinary key is ignored until the points are placed, like the archetype gate; the action-bar chips are inert behind both gates too. Unplaced points are bounded by `AbilityScores::headroom` (a point with no score below `SCORE_CAP` to go in is not owed, so the gate can always be satisfied), the offer is empty while dead (the corpse view and `r` win), and the screen collapses to one line a score when the area is too short for the full five-row layout.

### Active game keys

- Movement: `w/a/s/d`, `h/l` for west/east, and arrow keys for cardinal directions; `<` or `,` for up; `>` or `.` for down.
- The Matlatesh sea-gate into the Sundered Reaches requires `Bane of the King Who Was Promised Nothing` and uses the same transient two-step warning as the Frontier descent.
- The ash-gate down from Yssgar's Reaches chamber into Kaelmyr requires `Bane of Yssgar, the Sundering Deep` (`is_kaelmyr_gateway`, `KAELMYR_GATE_TITLE`) and uses the same transient two-step warning. It is the deepest end-game gate in the game.
- The first dungeon descent from Whisperwood into Duskhollow requires `Bane of the Elder Treant`.
- Living-dark entrances from the three capitals require `Bane of the Archdemon Mal'gareth`.
- The Town Square Frontier descent requires `Bane of the Archdemon Mal'gareth`, `Bane of The Bonewright Lich`, `Bane of the Elder Dryad`, and `Bane of the Abyss-Thing`; after those title gates, it still uses a transient two-step warning: the first `>` logs that the Frontier is older, meaner country for seasoned adventurers, and the next `>` confirms descent. Service-backed non-movement actions clear the pending warning.
- Combat: `space`, `x`, or Enter attacks when not in a list panel; `z` flees.
- Abilities: `1-9` use unlocked ability slots unless a list panel is open; `0` uses slot 10. The Abilities panel is a list panel: Enter casts the highlighted ability, which is the only way to reach rosters deeper than ten (the classic classes' late slots). In the Abilities panel `x` arms the highlighted ability for swapping; a second `x` on another row swaps the two (and `x` on the same row cancels). While a swap is armed, `r` drops the custom order and restores the natural unlock order. The order is stored as `ability_order` and persists.
- World actions: `y` works a resource node in the room (chop/mine/fish/forage/skin - the highest tier you qualify for); `u` opens the crafting panel where a craft station stands; `i` opens "the Ways" fast-travel menu when standing on a waystone portal (moved off `y`, which gather uses); `m` cycles the **map**: closed -> the graphical overhead field (`WORLDMAP.md`) -> the **land map** (`WORLDMAP.md`) -> closed, via `state::cycle_map` and `state::MapMode`. On a terminal too small for the land picture the cycle is two states, not three (field -> closed): the render pass hands the state `set_lands_available(lands_fit(body))` each frame the way it hands back `list_scroll`, and `cycle_map` skips the page, because one key that always does something beats a second press that silently falls back to the atlas; on the field page `x` marks the crosshair room as a destination and the room panel carries a `heading` line naming the next exit to take until you arrive, and `q` toggles the active-quest overlay (see `WORLDMAP.md`); below 32x10 it falls back to the text **World Atlas**: `World::region_progress` scores each of the `REGIONS` for visited/total rooms + boss count and flags the region the player stands in (`RegionProgress.here`, a `◈ you are here` marker), rendered as meters + `◆N` loot markers by `ui.rs::atlas_panel`); `r` recalls to Embergate's Town Square when out of combat; `;` **retreats to the nearest safe haven** (`svc::retreat_to_haven`: a BFS over walkable exits to the closest `safe` room, refusing mid-combat and never expanding through a progression gate the player's titles wouldn't pass, via the silent `gate_blocks` twin of `can_cross_progression_gate`) - deep in a maze it reads as "back to this zone's gate"; `f` toggles the Follow panel; `g` casts the Resurrection rite on the nearest fallen adventurer in the room (Cleric/Paladin/Druid only); `p` opens the Stable (companion vendor) where one stands; `q` opens the **Animal Taming** panel where a tameable wild beast roams (Enter attempts the tame); `n` opens the housing ledger (at the clerk, or inside a home you own); `e` opens the appearance/bio builder; `o` (Examine) on a quest board reads it out and opens its picker (`Panel::Board`, see `WORLD.md`, Board quests) instead of assigning a bounty blind. In the Inventory panel `A`/`C`/`J` batch-sell all loose gear / commons / non-upgrades (keeping worn gear and every `Consumable`/`Utility` item - poisons included, not just potions); inventory and shop rows show both a stat-delta line and a coloured `▲+N%` / `▼-N%` upgrade tag vs. what's worn, plus the item's own description line.
- Local chat: `'` opens a **say** compose line (`state.chat_buffer`; input capture runs at the top of `handle_key`, before the Esc-leaves check, so Esc cancels compose). Enter sends via the existing world-local `say` (room occupants, `LogKind::Say`); backspace edits; the prompt renders on a reserved bottom row in `draw_page`. **Lateania chat is world-local and never reaches late.sh's global feed** (`say` only `log_to`s in-world players; it does not publish to activity/#lounge).
- While dead (a corpse): all normal keys are suppressed; only `r`/Enter (release to the temple) and `Esc` (leave) respond, until a resurrection or the auto-release deadline.
- Panels: `c` character, `v` abilities, `t` inventory, `b` shop where a merchant exists, `o` examine/look, `k` titles, `j` quest journal (a list panel: Enter tracks/untracks the highlighted quest's target - it sets the same `map_dest` the map's `x` does, so the compass line guides to it), `f` follow, `!` leaderboard.
- List panels: `w/s` or up/down move cursor; `1-9` jump and activate; Enter activates. The view auto-scrolls to keep the highlighted row within a small scroll-off margin (top and bottom).
- Cursor-less text panels (character/leaderboard): `[` / `]` scroll. Both scroll offsets share one interior-mutable `list_scroll` on `state::State`, clamped to content by the render pass and reset on panel change.
- Inventory panel: `x` sells the selected inventory row when a shop is present.
- Follow panel: Enter follows/stops the selected in-room adventurer; `x` stops following whoever is currently followed, including absent/separated targets.
- `Esc` twice leaves active Lateania and returns to the character-select landing (`CONTEXT.md` §3).

### Panels

**The room panel's shape.** The rail, the standing keys, and the log strip all size to the terminal:
- **The rail scales with the screen.** `ui::side_width` is `total_width / 4`, clamped to `SIDE_WIDE`..`SIDE_MAX` (34..60), `SIDE_NARROW` below 84 columns: a fixed rail on a 190-column terminal spends a third of the panel's height on wrap damage while the main column sits half empty.
- **What is specific to the room leads the panel.** `room_actions` renders a `You can` block right under the vitals, in a louder style (`action_hint`, green key + bright label), for the keys that only work where you stand (`g` raise, `b` shop, `p` stable, `u` craft, `y` gather, `q` tame, `i` ways, `n` ledger, `o` board, `G` mend or rouse a hurt pet). Empty in a room with none of them. `footer_hints` is four dim compact lines of the standing keys plus `? all keys`, including the waypoint pair `: waypoint` and `/ warp`, while say (`'`), ranks (`!`) and leave (`Esc`) live only in the `?` guide (pinned by `what_this_room_offers_leads_the_panel_and_the_standing_keys_stay_short`); mid-fight it is just the four combat keys. The `?` guide (`help_modal/data.rs::lateania_help_lines`) is the one place the full key reference lives.
- **Nothing in the rail may run past its width.** `draw_room_side` paints pre-wrapped lines with no `Wrap`, so a line built without knowing the width is chopped at the edge (`f follow` rendered as `f follo`). The standing keys are therefore short `key label` chips packed to the measured width by `pack_hint_chips` (at most 8 lines, and fewer as the rail widens), and `action_hint` labels are kept short enough to fit the narrowest rail. Both the packer and anything sizing a chip against the rail subtract the same `RAIL_INDENT`. The one chip built from world data is the warp key: with a waypoint fixed it reads `/ warp <zone>` (`PlayerView::waypoint` carries the zone name, not a bare flag), trimmed with `truncate` to whatever the rail leaves it. That replaced a permanent `waypoint set (/ to warp)` line in the room panel, and took `f follow`'s chip slot; follow lives in the `?` guide (`help_modal::data`), which is where a key with nothing to report about its own state belongs. Pinned by the width sweep in `what_this_room_offers_leads_the_panel_and_the_standing_keys_stay_short`.
- **The bottom log strip grows with the terminal.** `ui::log_strip_height` is `height / 3` capped at 12, and always under half the height so the field keeps the larger share.
- **On a short terminal the events move into the rail instead** (`ui::events_in_rail`, under 24 rows). The full-width strip is the better shape when there are rows to spend on it - log lines are sentences, and sentences want width - but a phone held sideways is twenty rows, and there `height / 3` comes off the one thing that cannot be scrolled back to: the live field. Below the threshold the field takes the full height and `draw_log_strip` is handed the bottom third of the side rail (`ui::rail_log_height`, which always leaves the room summary the larger share), a column of wrapped text either way. `draw_room_side` is untouched by this: it measures its click rects against whatever rect it is handed, so the split happens in `draw_game` above it.
- **The standing keys are anchored to the floor of the rail, not trailing the content.** `room_panel` returns a `RoomPanel { body, footer, .. }`, and `draw_room_side` reserves the footer's wrapped-row count at the bottom (`ui::footer_anchor`) so the room's state reads top-down from the vitals and the key block sits at a fixed edge, instead of the panel ending wherever the content happened to stop with dead space below. The berth is only taken while it leaves the room at least half the rail; below that the footer goes back to being ordinary content at the end of the body, where it scrolls. `foe_hits`/`player_hits` index into `body` and their click rects are measured against `body_area`, not the whole panel.
- **A key shown in `You can` is not repeated in the standing keys.** `room_action_entries` is the single `(key, label)` table; `room_actions` renders it and `footer_hints` filters its chips against it, so `o` and `i` appear once, in the loud place. `o look / interact` is an entry in the table, not a hint inside the `Of note` list (`read the board` where a board is present, since `o` opens the look list and picking the board out of it opens the board).
- **`[` / `]` scroll every panel, always, with no conditions.** `State::scroll_up`/`scroll_down` branch on one thing: a panel with a cursor (`list_len() > 0`) scrolls by **walking its cursor** one row, since the render pass already keeps the selection in view and paging it drags the view along; a cursor-less panel (room, character sheet, atlas, leaderboard) shifts `list_scroll` directly and the render pass clamps it. There is deliberately no manual-vs-auto scroll mode: one source of truth for where a panel sits, so the keys are never a silent no-op.
- **The room panel scrolls too.** `draw_side` short-circuits to `draw_room_side` before reaching the shared scroll path, so `ui::scroll_room_panel` fits it: the vitals block (down to the first spacer) stays **pinned** so HP is never something you scroll to find, the rest scrolls on the same `[`/`]` + `list_scroll` cell every cursor-less panel uses, and a `+N more  ] to scroll` marker takes the last row when content is hidden. It is pure and unit-tested because the arithmetic is fiddly: it counts **wrapped rows, not logical lines**, and the marker row is only reserved when something is genuinely hidden (reserving it unconditionally would push the last line off the final page and report an unreachable `+1 more`). `draw_room_side` maps a line index to its screen row through `pinned`/`off` before recording click targets; getting that wrong aims clicks at the wrong foe.

- **A tutorial line that reprints forever is only a tutorial the first time.** An adoptable critter's `feed it daily (~) and it may take to you as a stray` cost two or three wrapped rows in every room that held one. `WildlifeView.adopt_streak` carries the courting progress, so the sentence shows until the first feed and then gives way to `(~ 2/5 days)`, which is shorter and says something the player does not already know. `adoptable` is also false once a stray is kept, because `~` stops courting critters then (`feed_target`) and the panel must not advertise a key that does nothing.

`state::Panel` variants:
- `Room`: current room, vitals, exits, mobs, occupants, wildlife, features, hints. The zone line carries the zone's derived mob-level band (`zone_with_band`, from `World::zone_band` - computed at seed time from the spawns, so it can't drift). Foe rows wrap the full name with a wider meter + numbers (plus `bleed xN`/`stunned` tags) on their own line. **In the field layout, while a fight is on** (a targeted mob or duel occupant), `draw_room_side` swaps the room summary for `battle_side_panel`: vitals, a Battle section (the locked foe's full name, wide meter, rank · attack school · weak/resist, afflictions), your `shield/empowered/stunned` effects, your companion with its auto-skills, the **full ability roster** (slot key, name, cost, effect *with the school it lands in*, ready/dim - the detail the bottom action bar's 7-char chips have no room for), the room's other foes under "Also here", and the combat keys. Rows carry their own click actions (`Vec<(usize, ClickAction)>`): foe rows switch the lock, ability rows cast, same as their keys. The classic layout keeps the room summary here because its main column already swaps to `battle_context`.
- `Character`: class, trait, scores, stats, titles, resurrection charges.
- `Abilities`: unlocked abilities, cost/readiness/effect.
- `Inventory`: pack items plus equipped items as rows. Enter on a row is context-sensitive via `state::inv_action`: worn gear comes **off** (`unequip_task`), loose gear goes on, a consumable is used. Selling checks the `equipped` row specifically, not the item id: a worn item refuses with a reason, but a loose duplicate of that same item id sells fine while the worn copy stays on (`svc::sell`). **On terminals ≥ 100x20 it expands to a full-screen master-detail layout** (`draw_inventory_screen`, the shop screen's twin): one line a piece (name, sell value, the `▲/▼` tag or `worn`) under the collapsible headers on the left, and on the right the highlighted piece against what is worn in its slot (`InvView::worn_name`/`worn_stats`), its description, what Enter does to it (`inv_action`), and whether `x` sells it here. The sell keys are only offered as live when `view.shop` is present. The pack covers the field and is the panel opened mid-fight, so its title line carries HP, the class resource, and `fighting <foe>` (`pack_vitals_spans`), ahead of the counts so a long line clips the purse, not the fight. Both screens build their rows and detail through the shared `item_row_line`/`item_detail_lines`, so they cannot drift apart. The sidebar `inventory_panel` serves anything smaller.
- `Map`: two pages on one key, chosen by `state::MapMode`. `Field` is the graphical overhead world map (`WORLDMAP.md`); `Lands` is the land map (`WORLDMAP.md`). `map_fits` is **32x10** (`MAP_MIN_W`/`MAP_MIN_H`) - the floor the map *body* needs, not the chrome around it, which `MapChrome` sheds instead (`WORLDMAP.md`). `lands_fit` is still 76x12, because the land picture is a fixed 76x13 drawing that cannot be shrunk; below that the page is not offered at all rather than degraded (see `m` under Active game keys). The text atlas in the side panel is what is left under 32x10, a thin band above compact mode.
- `Shop`: merchant stock if `shop_at(room)` exists. **On terminals ≥ 100x20 it expands to a full-screen master-detail layout** (`draw_shop_screen`: the stock left at one line per item - name, price, upgrade tag - under the same collapsible category headers, and the highlighted piece right, stood directly against what it would replace: its stats, the worn item's name and stats, the delta, the overall percent, and the buy prompt); the sidebar `shop_panel` serves anything smaller. The side rail renders every listing as four wrapped lines, which a ten-item shop already outgrows and the market tier's stock outgrows badly. Same rows, same cursor, same keys in both renderings. `draw_shop_screen` takes its data (rows, `ShopView`, cursor, purse) rather than the `State`, so the layout renders in a test without a service behind it. Entries arrive **sorted best-first within each category** (by `Item::power`, then price) from the `ShopView` builder, not from the authored stock order. Row cells are fixed-width: sizing the name from a row's own price/tag once clipped `▲+77%` down to `▲+7`.
- `Examine`: room features; fountains can restore vitals.
- `Titles`: earned titles; selecting active title again clears it.
- `Quests`: the journal - the active starter step, accepted bounties, the Long Road, and (once `frontier_open`) the Frontier zone list. A list panel whose cursor walks the quests *and then the Long Road milestones* (`list_len` = quests + road), so w/s can scroll the whole panel even with a single active quest; Enter tracks the highlighted quest's `target` - or a crown's lair (`RoadStepView.target`, resolved once at world build by `road_targets` from the boss spawn's home room) - on the compass/map. The key hints (`w/s` move, Enter track, `j` close) sit at the *top* of the panel - the Long Road makes it the longest panel in the game, so bottom hints scrolled out of sight. **On terminals ≥ 100x20 the journal expands to a full-screen three-column layout** (`draw_journal_screen`: In progress | The Long Road | The Frontier, each column scrolled to keep the cursor visible) following the character-sheet pattern; the sidebar `quests_panel` serves anything smaller. Same cursor indices and keys in both renderings.
- `Board`: **on terminals ≥ 100x20 the board expands to a full-screen master-detail layout** (`draw_board_screen`: the postings list left, the highlighted posting's full story - blurb, task, hint, reward, sealed/READY state - right); the sidebar `board_panel` serves anything smaller.
- `Follow`: current occupants, follow target tag, stop-follow action.
- `Crafting`: recipes worked at the craft station(s) in the room; select and Enter to craft. **On terminals ≥ 100x20 it expands to a full-screen master-detail layout** (`draw_craft_screen`, the shop's and the pack's third twin): one line a recipe on the left (what it makes, a readiness cell, the `▲/▼` tag) under the collapsible skill headers, and on the right the piece it would make stood against what is worn in its slot through the same `ItemDetail` the shop uses, then the **ingredient checklist** (`✓`/`✗` per line with what is in the pack against what it costs), the trade gate (`Smithing 12 · needs 8 · +70 xp`), and what Enter will do. The readiness cell is the one thing a maker scanning the list wants and it sits where the shop puts its price: `ready`, `lvl N` for a trade gate, or `1/2` for the ingredient lines covered. The sidebar `crafting_panel` serves anything smaller and carries the output's stat line too, because a name plus a pile of ingredients never once said what came out of them. Entries arrive **best-first within each trade** (by `Item::power`, then level gate), sorted per station; the sections follow `CraftSkill::PANEL_ORDER`, which puts **Alchemy first** (the most-worked station) and the rest in `ALL` order. Stock goods (draughts, oils, poisons, food) show how many are already in the pack: `×N` in the list's tag cell (gear keeps its `▲/▼` tag there), `N in your pack` in the detail pane, `×N` after the name in the sidebar.
- `Taming`: the tameable wild beasts roaming the room (Broceliande), each with its required Animal Taming level and your odds; select and Enter to attempt a tame.
- `Rail`: the room rail (`draw_room_side` in field mode: the room summary, or the battle frame mid-fight) drawn full screen at any size. Toggled with `=`, or a tap on the narrow layout's `= more` chip; `x` still attacks from it. It is the phone layout's way to everything the rail carries (exits, wildlife, pet, standing keys) without a second rail layout.
- `Leaderboard`: read-only, scrollable with `[`/`]`, opened/closed with `!` (not `?`, which late.sh reserves globally across every door game for a cross-door help overlay - a Lateania binding on `?` is intercepted before the door's own `handle_key` ever runs). Top ten currently-connected, classed adventurers by level, lifetime pvp kills, and total gold (carried + banked). Built once per `snapshot()` (`WorldState::build_leaderboard`, identical for every viewer) and shared into every `PlayerView` via `Arc<LeaderboardView>` rather than recomputed/cloned per player. This is a **live, in-session** ranking over `self.players`, not a persistent all-time record - an offline character's best-ever level doesn't appear once they log out. Unclassed characters never appear on any board.


UI uses a two-column layout. The compact fallback (`draw_compact`) takes terminals narrower than 50 columns or shorter than 9 rows, except that with RPG mode on the Room panel checks for the narrow field layout first (any width under 96 with 8+ rows, see below), so there compact applies only under 8 rows. The left column splits current room context (`Now`) from newest-first action scrollback (`Recent`) with a visible divider; **while a fight is on** (a `targeted` mob, or a duel via a `targeted` occupant) the `Now` block is replaced by the **battle frame** (`ui::battle_context`): the foe's full name, its nature line (rank · attack school · weakness/resist, from `MobView.school/weak/resist`), wide HP meters for both sides, `afflicted:` (DoT stacks/stun) and the player's `shield/empowered/stunned` effects (`PlayerView` fields), reverting the moment the fight ends. Note the battle frame lives in the *classic* layout's left column only, and with RPG mode on the Room panel never draws that layout (every width 8+ rows tall gets a field layout, see below): on 96+ columns the fight reads from the side panel's battle frame (`battle_side_panel`, see the Room panel note above); the narrow field layout has no side panel, so there the same battle frame takes the centre of the screen in place of the field (see below). RPG mode off keeps the classic layout and the battle frame at every width from 50 columns up. The `Now` region wraps the room description naturally and only truncates the whole context as a last resort to preserve recent-event space. Service room-description lines use `LogKind::Room` and are filtered out of `Recent` so movement does not bury combat, loot, chat, and system events. Arrivals use compact `LogKind::Travel` breadcrumbs so Recent still shows where the player has just been. Consecutive identical recent events are collapsed with an `xN` suffix so repeated blocked-movement warnings do not flood the split.
**The map is never in the side rail.** With RPG mode on, the Room panel is the live field at every width: 96+ columns get field + room rail + full-width log strip; narrower (a phone) gets `ui::draw_narrow_field` - a one-row status bar (`narrow_status_line`: HP, resource, room name, and a tappable `= more` chip on the right that opens `Panel::Rail`), the field across the full width in the centre, then up to three **here rows** right above a small event feed (`narrow_log_height`, `height / 4` clamped 3..7): one foe row (`narrow_foes_line`: each foe's level, name and HP, each a tap target that locks onto it, a `+N` tail for the ones that do not fit) and up to two `you can` rows (`narrow_action_lines`: the rail's `room_action_entries` packed as `key label` chips). The here rows only take rows the field can spare above a 6-row floor. The field draws no colour key at any width: the header row and the field itself are all it paints. **Mid-fight the field gives way to the battle frame** (`draw_narrow_battle`), built from the same blocks as the wide rail's `battle_side_panel` (`battle_frame_block`, `battle_abilities_block`, `battle_others_block`, `battle_keys_block`): from 60 columns (a phone held sideways) frame + other foes sit on the left and abilities + combat keys on the right, so a 60x17 body shows vitals, the foe's nature, and a six-ability roster at once; narrower, the blocks stack as in the rail, and `Also here` is what clips. The event feed stays under it either way. `draw_narrow_field` returns its click rects rather than recording them, so it renders under test without a `State` (pinned by `a_phone_gets_the_field_with_the_foes_and_room_keys_above_the_events` and `a_phone_mid_fight_trades_the_field_for_the_full_battle_frame`). The classic (RPG mode off) layout draws no map in its rail either; `m` opens the overhead map.
Room-panel variable text rows (zone, exits, features, foes, occupants, wildlife) should use the side wrapping helpers in `ui.rs` so long labels wrap within the side column instead of clipping against the border.
Non-Room side panels are rendered through `side_paragraph`, which enables Ratatui wrapping for long quest, inventory, shop, title, and ability rows.

### Appearance and portrait

`appearance::portrait(class_key, sel) -> Vec<String>` (`PORTRAIT_ROWS`): a composed ASCII bust assembled from the player's own Build/Hair/Eyes/Bearing choices plus a class-flavoured headpiece (helm/hood/circlet/laurel/wild-band by class key) - pure glyph rows, coloured by `ui.rs::composed_portrait` with the class accent + per-feature tints. Shown on the character sheet (`sheet_identity`), when profiling another adventurer (Follow panel, keyed by their snapshot `class_key`/`appearance_idx`), and live in the `e` appearance builder as a preview. Snapshot carries `class_key`/`appearance_idx` on `PlayerView` and `OccupantView` (lock-free/snapshot-only).

### The world map

The overhead field (`Map` → `Field`) and the land map (`Map` → `Lands`): chrome shedding, geometry and safeguards, POI arrows, stairs, the authored atlas. All in `WORLDMAP.md`.
