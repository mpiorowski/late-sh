# Realm Context

## Metadata
- Scope: `late-ssh/src/app/door/realm`: the house's one standing world, multiplayer (2-10) simultaneous-turn territory conquest, one season at a time, on real Earth or a drawn world. Plus persistence in `late-core/src/models/realm_game.rs`, migrations 192-198 and 241-243, the map generator `tools/realm-mapgen`, and the committed assets `late-ssh/assets/realm/`.
- Domain: a long-lived (weeks) DB-durable game the server opens, numbered as a season, with the next one rising when it falls. Each player gets N action points per *realm day*; an action (claim a free territory, attack an owned one) **resolves the instant it is taken** and everyone sees it on their next snapshot. Races are first-come-first-served through the row CAS. A realm day rolls over at the world's `reset_hour_utc` (`HOUSE_RESET_HOUR_UTC`, 18) and only governs the budget and the idle clock. Win = conquering every rival (or, the same finish from the other end, holding every territory). Unclaimed land does not keep a realm open.
- Parent context: the root `CONTEXT.md` (the Games hub, `door/hub`, is the card it is entered from; the live strip, `app/live`, is where its moments show).
- Companion docs:
  - `RULES.md`: moves, reach and routes, walls, options, protection phases, budgets and banking, decay, pace, winning, payouts, ranking.
  - `MAPS.md`: the Earth atlas and its generator, and drawn worlds (`mapgen.rs`).
  - `UI.md`: the hub card, joining, the world's chat, the live strip, watching, the board's five views, and the history slider.
- Status: Active.

## 2. Shape

Three-layer split, daily-domain style (no live actor per game, load-validate-persist, everything survives restarts):

- **Rules as data.** `rulesets.rs::RULESETS` is the roster; every mechanic knob (min/max players, actions/day tiers by player count, probability constants, distance decay) is a `RealmRuleset` field. The **map is not**: it is picked per season (`WorldChoice`, Earth or drawn) and frozen into the snapshot beside the rules, because the same rules on another world is another game. A game freezes a serde `RealmRulesetSnapshot` into its state at the opening, so editing a ruleset never mutates running games. New rulesets = new array entries, never new branches. New fields carry `#[serde(default)]` so already-frozen games still parse.
- **Instant engine.** `resolver.rs` owns the persisted state shape (`RealmGameState` in `realm_games.state` JSONB) and the pure `apply_action(state, map, actor, action, day, seed)`. One action = one point = one roll, applied on the spot. Seed = `blake3(game_id ‖ day ‖ revision ‖ actor ‖ target)`; RNG is a local SplitMix64 (portable forever). Because the revision is in the seed, a retry after a lost CAS is a genuinely new roll against the world as it actually is. Illegal actions (`ActionRejected`) are **refused**, not fizzled: nothing is spent, nothing is logged, the player is told and picks again. `sweep_idle` is the only other engine entry point (the abandoned-realm dissolve, `RULES.md`).
- **`RealmState::tick()` returns `RealmTick { banner, changed }`**, and `changed` is the load-bearing half. A realm action resolves in a service task, so the event comes back on one frame and the reloaded board on a later one; without `changed` the second frame would wait for the next keypress. It is set by a new snapshot, any event, a board or log load landing, and the muster countdown crossing a second.
- **The house runs the season loop.** Nobody creates a realm: the sweeper, on every replica, opens the next world whenever none is open or active (`RealmService::open_season` → `open_world(house_spec)`). **The opening is claimed before anything is spent on it** (root CONTEXT once-only rule): `open_world` takes `RealmGame::try_lock_season_opening`, a transaction-scoped advisory lock, and under it rechecks that no world stands, reads the season (`next_season`), builds the spec, pays the AI for a drawn world's names, and inserts, all in one transaction. A replica that loses the claim, or finds a world standing, spends nothing and reads `RealmSeasonOpen::Standing` (counted in `late_ssh_realm_seasons_total`); an opening that dies half way rolls back and leaves no claim and no row. The insert lands the row **active** (`RealmGame::open_season`), so nothing can strand an `open` row that blocks every season after it, and the table holds the invariant on its own (`realm_games_one_live`, a partial unique index). The row is the `system` user's (`SYSTEM_FINGERPRINT`; a tick before the lounge feed ensured it bails and the next tick opens the world) with an empty roster. `house_spec` is the whole product choice in one place: the standard rules at `HOUSE_PACE` (`fast`), `HOUSE_RESET_HOUR_UTC`, Earth for odd seasons and a drawn world (`GeneratedMapSpec::default`: 5 continents, 8 islands, 240 territories) for even ones. Tests open their own `WorldSpec` through the same `open_world`. The name reads "Season N" (`game_name`).
- **Service.** `svc.rs::RealmService`, process-global (constructed in `main.rs` beside `DailyService`): watch snapshot + broadcast events + fire-and-forget mutator tasks + the notify worker + the 60s sweeper. `act()` is the hot path: load, roll, persist under the row CAS, archive the log line, emit `ActionResolved` (targeted, the actor's banner), republish the snapshot. The sweeper opens the next season when no world stands, gives a roomless world its chat room, rolls the world's day over at its `reset_hour_utc` (archiving the day it closes), dissolves an abandoned realm, settles the pot of any finished game whose `payout` is still NULL, and deletes the chat rooms of worlds that ended over 30 days ago.
- **`news` is what the live strip reads** (`RealmGameState::news`, `RealmNews { at, kind }`): the write that made a moment stamps it into the row, so every replica's strip shows the same moment at the same instant (`live.rs`, `LiveSource::Realm`). The moments: `Opened`, `Joined` (who, in which colour), `Took` (who took which country, by name, from whom), `Fell` (who lost the last of their land, to whom), `Ended`. A claim on empty land is not a moment; a fall outranks the take that caused it; the end outranks everything (`news_of_action`, `ended_news`). Usernames are carried in the news because a withdrawal removes the player from the roster.
- **Fan-out is derived from the database, on every replica** (root CONTEXT multi-replica rule). A trigger on `realm_games` (migration 241) fires `realm_game_changed` on every row write; `RealmService::start_notify_worker` re-reads the snapshot on each notify (`Refresh::RealmGames`, resync retried through `read_until_ok`). `publish` diffs the old snapshot against the new and emits: `GameChanged` (moved revision, changed status, payout or `chat_room_id`, or a row that left the listing), `DayRolled` (`last_resolved_day` advanced on an active game), `GameFinished` (left the active list for the finished one), `SeasonOpened` (a new row still in its muster). Publishes are serialised under `publish_lock`, and an unseeded snapshot derives nothing, so a resync after a reconnect does not announce every game as new. Only `ActionResolved` and `Error` are sent by the writer, since their session is on the writing replica. What must happen once (the #lounge headlines, the lounge lines, the payout) stays with the write that won the CAS. The 60s sweeper refresh is the backstop.

## 3. Persistence (late-core)

- `realm_games` (migration 192; `reset_hour_utc` 195, `name` 196, the `realm_game_changed` trigger 241, `payout` 242, `season` and the one-live index 243): status open/active/finished/cancelled, `ruleset_id`, `reset_hour_utc`, `last_resolved_day` (the last realm day the sweeper closed out, DB-durable, never an in-process timer), `chat_room_id` (`ON DELETE SET NULL`), state JSONB with top-level `revision` CAS (`stored_revision_eq_sql`, daily_match pattern), written by every action, which is what makes the first-come-first-served race honest. `season` counts the worlds the house opened (0 on rows from before it did); `name` stays blank for house worlds and `game_name` reads "Season N".
- `realm_days` (193, + `board` in 198): the per-day log archive. A day's row is rewritten as the day is played (`archive_day` upserts), so history survives a process dying mid-day; the game row keeps only the current day in `state.last_day`. Paged by the log view; the closing board is `UI.md`'s history.
- `realm_game.rs` methods: `try_lock_season_opening`, `open_season`, `next_season`, `live` (the one open or active row), `list_open/active/finished_recent(24h)`, `list_active_for_sweep(now, limit)` (the "is this game behind its own clock" test is in the SQL, from `reset_hour_utc`, so the per-tick bound only lands on games with work to do), `list_finished_unpaid`, `set_payout`, `attach_chat_room` (`ChatRoom::get_or_create_realm_room`; idempotent), `delete_stale_chat_rooms` (30 days after a world ended, voice channels with them; the `DailyMatch` shape), `update_state_cas`, `advance_day_cas`, `finish_cas`, `cancel`, `archive_day`, `load_day_logs`. `REALM_GAME_CHANGED_CHANNEL` is the notify channel the model owns.
- **The snapshot never builds a world.** `game_item` takes the territory total from `map::territory_total` (the spec's own count for a generated map, the static atlas for a fixed one), so re-listing every game after every action is a parse, not a generation. A row whose state no longer parses is left out, logged and counted (`record_realm_snapshot_row_rejected`) the first time it goes missing on this replica, the daily `SnapshotRowError` shape.

## 4. Critical invariants

- An action is resolved exactly once, by the write that wins the row CAS. The retry after a lost CAS re-rolls against the reloaded state (the revision is in the seed); it never replays the old roll.
- A *refused* action changes nothing: no point, no log line, no state write. Only rolls are history.
- The projected percentage on screen and the rolled percentage come from the same `action_probability` call on the same state. If they ever diverge, the game is lying to players.
- Days are DB integers derived from the game's own `reset_hour_utc` (`svc::realm_day`); never an in-process timer, and never a global midnight.
- The engine (`resolver.rs`, `map.rs`, `rulesets.rs`) stays pure: no db/tokio imports; `svc.rs` owns all IO. The muster freeze is the one rule outside it (`RULES.md`).
- `state.revision` only grows; CAS losers reload.
- Nothing a session is told about another player's write comes from the process that wrote it: `GameChanged`, `DayRolled`, `GameFinished` and `SeasonOpened` are derived from the snapshot diff after a re-read, so they are the same on every replica. A new fan-out event is derived there too, never sent from a mutation.
- At most one world is open or active at any moment, and the table holds that, not the sweeper: `realm_games_one_live`. A season is opened by exactly one replica's tick, the one holding the opening claim; the others spend nothing and read `Standing`.
- The strip's stamp for a moment is the row's own `news.at`, written by the write that made it, never a replica's clock at read time.
- A finished game's pot is settled from the stored row and recorded in `realm_games.payout`; a NULL there means the sweeper still owes it.
- Ruleset snapshots are frozen at the opening; never resolve against the live `RULESETS` entry. New ruleset fields need `#[serde(default)]`.
- Asset format: readers must keep ignoring unknown trailing planes in `earth_grid.bin` so terrain can land later without a version break.
- `LogEntry::Fizzled` and `FizzleReason` are retired but must stay in the enum: archived days from the queue era still deserialize through them.

## 5. Tests

- **Playouts (`simulation_test.rs`)**: whole games against the real engine, seeded so a failure is reproducible. Two, five and eight players with mixed policies (settler / warlord / cautious / absentee) run to a winner; every day checks the invariants that must never break (land held by the dead, a budget overspent or over its ceiling, decay past its floor, active days exceeding days elapsed, odds outside the ruleset's bounds, an exit without an exit day). Also: the join window staying open at least a week, a latecomer coming out of protection with a position, an absentee surviving but falling behind, two pure turtles never winning, every pace resolving in order, and end-to-end determinism. Observed with the current knobs: **2p resolves in ~47 days, 5p in ~60, 8p in ~79**; the map fills around day 26 (5p) to 37 (8p); the doors to newcomers close around day 8-9. The tests print those numbers (`--nocapture`), so a ruleset change that shifts the feel of the game shows up as a changed line rather than a silent drift.
- Engine (`resolver_test.rs`): points spent per attempt, budgets and refills, refusals for free, the first-come-first-served race, reach and its collapse in the odds, long strikes, an island start sailing instead of being stranded, elimination and last-one-standing, the abandoned-realm dissolve, day rollover, seed determinism (and a retry re-rolling), ranking tie-breaks, the opening truce and its countdown, walls priced as they stand on the day of the roll, the bank never shrinking with a longer absence, a realm short of a rival keeping its doors open.
- Rules (`rulesets.rs` inline): roster lookup, probability clamps and monotonicity, the distance collapse never passing 1%, crossing a rival costing more than going round, a short sea crossing costing less than a march, options as data, a bigger defender beating the linear expectation, tier scaling, snapshot serde.
- Maps: `map.rs` inline (testmap shape, earth asset consistency), `map_ui.rs` inline (zoom range, sampled level never coarser than the scale, bounding boxes), `mapgen_test.rs` (same spec same world, a playable map at every size, Earth-sized land whatever the parameters, islands apart from the mainland, clamped parameters, names topped up from the pool, the cache, continents that are not discs).
- Screens: `history_ui_test.rs` (the map painted from the day on screen, day one first, unpositioned days skipped, a half-read run not claiming its start, the slider's ends), `targets_ui_test.rs` (the selected row filled edge to edge).
- Service (`svc_test.rs`, real `test_db`, plus inline tests in `svc.rs`): the season loop (one world at a time, Earth and drawn worlds alternating, another replica's opening left to it, the muster), joins and spawns, actions landing in the live log and the archive, the budget on the game's own hour, the sweeper's rollover, dissolve, payout settlement (and a failed payout paid once on retry), cross-replica events through a re-read, corrupt rows leaving the listing, each world's room and its 30-day reap, withdrawals, the lounge headlines and calls, the anti-alt pot.
- App flow (`app/input_flow_test.rs`): the chat column, the board as a backtick stop, Esc peeling order.
- Run them scoped: `make test-llm ARGS="-p late-ssh -E 'test(realm)'"`.

## 6. Deferred / future hooks

There is no dev key that moves a game through days: a realm pays real chips,
and such a key would mint them. Multi-day rules are exercised in tests
(`svc::act_at`/`sweep` take the clock, and `open_the_doors` backdates a row).

- Terrain plane (mountain/forest/desert) in the grid asset; the format already reserves it (`flags` bit0).
- Sea adjacency as a first-class relation (an oversea target is priced from km, which is close enough and needs no asset change), custom/fantasy maps (data-only: new asset pair + `map_by_id` arm + a ruleset), more rulesets (banked points, different decay curves, a no-distance-penalty variant), spectator count, sidebar-panel presence, a voice channel beside the world's chat.
