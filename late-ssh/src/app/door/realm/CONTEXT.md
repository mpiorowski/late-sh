# Realm Context

## Metadata
- Scope: `late-ssh/src/app/door/realm`: the house's one standing world, multiplayer (2-20) territory conquest, one season at a time, on real Earth or a drawn world. Plus persistence in `late-core/src/models/realm_game.rs`, migrations 192-198, 241-243 and 272, the map generator `tools/realm-mapgen`, and the committed assets `late-ssh/assets/realm/`.
- Domain: a long-lived (weeks) DB-durable game the server opens, numbered as a season, with the next one rising when it falls. Each player gets N points per *realm day*, and the ruleset's engine decides what a point buys (`RULES.md`). The house plays the **campaign** (`Resolution::Orders`): points go on secret orders by day, and the **night** resolves everyone's together when the day turns over at the world's `reset_hour_utc` (`HOUSE_RESET_HOUR_UTC`, 18); a world is won by dominating a share of it for three nights, by holding the most when the season clock runs out, or by being the last one standing. The **standard** ruleset (`Resolution::Instant`), frozen for the world that was standing when the campaign shipped, resolves each action the instant it is taken, first-come-first-served through the row CAS, and is won by conquering every rival. Unclaimed land keeps neither open.
- Parent context: the root `CONTEXT.md` (the Games hub, `door/hub`, is the card it is entered from; the live strip, `app/live`, is where its moments show).
- Companion docs:
  - `RULES.md`: orders and the night (the campaign), moves, reach and routes (standard), walls, options, protection phases, budgets and banking, decay, pace, winning, payouts, ranking.
  - `MAPS.md`: the Earth atlas and its generator, and drawn worlds (`mapgen.rs`).
  - `UI.md`: the hub card, joining, the world's chat, the live strip, watching, the board's six views, the order prompt, the dispatch, the colours, and the history slider and its replay.
- Status: Active.

## 2. Shape

Three-layer split, daily-domain style (no live actor per game, load-validate-persist, everything survives restarts):

- **Rules as data.** `rulesets.rs::RULESETS` is the roster; every mechanic knob (min/max players, actions/day tiers by player count, probability constants, distance decay, the night's force and defence factors, the domination bar, the season clock) is a `RealmRuleset` field. The **map is not**: it is picked per season (`WorldChoice`, Earth or drawn) and frozen into the snapshot beside the rules, because the same rules on another world is another game. A game freezes a serde `RealmRulesetSnapshot` into its state at the opening, so editing a ruleset never mutates running games. New rulesets = new array entries, never new branches, with one exception: `resolution: Resolution { Instant, Orders }` picks the engine, and it is matched exhaustively in exactly three places, nowhere else: `svc::close_day` (what turning the day over does), the service entry points (`act_at` refuses on `Orders`; `place_order_at` and `withdraw_order_at`, through `write_order`, refuse on `Instant`), and `RealmState` (what `a`/`f` do, how a target row is priced, whether the history offers the replay). Every view draws what `RealmState` hands it (`TargetRow`'s `Route` and `Lock`), and the log, archive, fan-out, news and results card are shared. New fields carry `#[serde(default)]` so already-frozen games still parse.
- **Orders engine.** `night.rs` is pure like the resolver. `place_order` validates on the spot (`OrderRejected`, refused for free) and spends the points; `withdraw_order` refunds them. `resolve_night(state, map)` closes the open day (`state.last_day`) with that day's seed: walls up, takes re-checked, every fight priced and rolled before any lands, falls, then `check_end_orders` once; the night's entries go on the day's log and on `state.last_night`. `projection` is the one pricing path for the board and the night (`RULES.md` "Orders").
- **One place turns a day over: `svc::close_day`.** The sweeper calls it for every game behind its own clock; on the campaign every order (`write_order`) calls it first and reads back what it did (`DayTurn`): the day on the row is the order's day (always the row's open day, never the clock the order was given under: around the hour the row can turn between the two, and an order spent on the day before would rewind its player's budget), a night ran (this replica's or another's) so the order lands on the day it opened after a fresh read, or the night failed and the order is refused once, with the sweeper left to run it. Instant: `close_instant_day` rolls the day and runs `sweep_idle`. Orders: `close_night`, inside the `realm.night` span, takes the opening board, runs the night, takes the closing board, rolls the day and runs `dissolve_if_abandoned`, then writes it all in one `write_turn` (`advance_day_cas`, or `finish_cas` when the night ended the game) against the revision it loaded. **A night runs once, inside the CAS that advances the day**: a replica that loses the write discards its night, archives nothing and announces nothing, and the winner's is the only one anyone sees. Only after the write wins does it archive the day with both boards, announce the call, and count `late_ssh_realm_nights_total` and `late_ssh_realm_fights_total`. A failed night is logged and counted once, in `close_day`, and leaves the row as it was, for the next sweep.
- **Instant engine.** `resolver.rs` owns the persisted state shape (`RealmGameState` in `realm_games.state` JSONB) and the pure `apply_action(state, map, actor, action, day, seed)`. One action = one point = one roll, applied on the spot. Seed = `blake3(game_id ‖ day ‖ revision ‖ actor ‖ target)`; RNG is a local SplitMix64 (portable forever). Because the revision is in the seed, a retry after a lost CAS is a genuinely new roll against the world as it actually is. Illegal actions (`ActionRejected`) are **refused**, not fizzled: nothing is spent, nothing is logged, the player is told and picks again. `sweep_idle` is the only other engine entry point (the abandoned-realm dissolve, `RULES.md`).
- **`RealmState::tick()` returns `RealmTick { banner, changed }`**, and `changed` is the load-bearing half. A realm action resolves in a service task, so the event comes back on one frame and the reloaded board on a later one; without `changed` the second frame would wait for the next keypress. It is set by a new snapshot, any event, a board or log load landing, the night's dispatch going up, a playing replay stepping on, and the muster countdown crossing a second.
- **The house runs the season loop.** Nobody creates a realm: the sweeper, on every replica, opens the next world whenever none is open or active (`RealmService::open_season` → `open_world(house_spec)`). **The opening is claimed before anything is spent on it** (root CONTEXT once-only rule): `open_world` takes `RealmGame::try_lock_season_opening`, a transaction-scoped advisory lock, and under it rechecks that no world stands, reads the season (`next_season`), builds the spec, pays the AI for a drawn world's names, and inserts, all in one transaction. A replica that loses the claim, or finds a world standing, spends nothing and reads `RealmSeasonOpen::Standing` (counted in `late_ssh_realm_seasons_total`); an opening that dies half way rolls back and leaves no claim and no row. The insert lands the row **active** (`RealmGame::open_season`), so nothing can strand an `open` row that blocks every season after it, and the table holds the invariant on its own (`realm_games_one_live`, a partial unique index). The row is the `system` user's (`SYSTEM_FINGERPRINT`; a tick before the lounge feed ensured it bails and the next tick opens the world) with an empty roster. `house_spec` is the whole product choice in one place: the campaign rules (`house_ruleset`) at `HOUSE_PACE` (`fast`), `HOUSE_RESET_HOUR_UTC`, Earth for odd seasons and a drawn world (`GeneratedMapSpec::default`: 5 continents, 8 islands, 300 territories) for even ones. Tests open their own `WorldSpec` through the same `open_world`. The name reads "Season N" (`game_name`).
- **Service.** `svc.rs::RealmService`, process-global (constructed in `main.rs` beside `DailyService`): watch snapshot + broadcast events + fire-and-forget mutator tasks + the notify worker + the 60s sweeper. On the standard ruleset `act()` is the hot path: load, roll, persist under the row CAS, archive the log line, emit `ActionResolved` (targeted, the actor's banner), republish the snapshot. On the campaign `place_order`/`withdraw_order` load, validate through `night`, persist under the row CAS and emit `OrderPlaced`/`OrderWithdrawn` to the actor alone (`late_ssh_realm_orders_total`); nothing is archived, since an order is not history until the night. The sweeper opens the next season when no world stands, gives a roomless world its chat room, turns the world's day over at its `reset_hour_utc` (`close_day`, archiving the day it closes), dissolves an abandoned realm, settles the pot of any finished game whose `payout` is still NULL, and deletes the chat rooms of worlds that ended over 30 days ago.
- **`news` is what the live strip reads** (`RealmGameState::news`, `RealmNews { at, kind }`): the write that made a moment stamps it into the row, so every replica's strip shows the same moment at the same instant (`live.rs`, `LiveSource::Realm`). The moments: `Opened`, `Joined` (who, in which colour), `Took` (who took which country, by name, from whom), `Fell` (who lost the last of their land, to whom), `Ended`. A claim on empty land is not a moment; a fall outranks the take that caused it; the end outranks everything (`news_of_action`, `ended_news`). A night is one moment at most, its biggest: the end, else a fall, else the heaviest take from a holder (`news_of_night`). Usernames are carried in the news because a withdrawal removes the player from the roster.
- **Fan-out is derived from the database, on every replica** (root CONTEXT multi-replica rule). A trigger on `realm_games` (migration 241) fires `realm_game_changed` on every row write; `RealmService::start_notify_worker` re-reads the snapshot on each notify (`Refresh::RealmGames`, resync retried through `read_until_ok`). `publish` diffs the old snapshot against the new and emits: `GameChanged` (moved revision, changed status, payout or `chat_room_id`, or a row that left the listing), `DayRolled` (`last_resolved_day` advanced on an active game), `GameFinished` (left the active list for the finished one), `SeasonOpened` (a new row still in its muster). Publishes are serialised under `publish_lock`, and an unseeded snapshot derives nothing, so a resync after a reconnect does not announce every game as new. Only `ActionResolved`, `OrderPlaced`, `OrderWithdrawn` and `Error` are sent by the writer, since their session is on the writing replica. What must happen once (the #lounge headlines, the lounge lines, the payout) stays with the write that won the CAS. The 60s sweeper refresh is the backstop.

## 3. Persistence (late-core)

- `realm_games` (migration 192; `reset_hour_utc` 195, `name` 196, the `realm_game_changed` trigger 241, `payout` 242, `season` and the one-live index 243): status open/active/finished/cancelled, `ruleset_id`, `reset_hour_utc`, `last_resolved_day` (the last realm day the sweeper closed out, DB-durable, never an in-process timer), `chat_room_id` (`ON DELETE SET NULL`), state JSONB with top-level `revision` CAS (`stored_revision_eq_sql`, daily_match pattern), written by every action, which is what makes the first-come-first-served race honest. `season` counts the worlds the house opened (0 on rows from before it did); `name` stays blank for house worlds and `game_name` reads "Season N".
- `realm_days` (193, + `board` in 198, + `board_open` in 272): the per-day log archive. A day's row is rewritten as the day is played (`archive_day` upserts, each board kept by `COALESCE` when a write carries none), so history survives a process dying mid-day; the game row keeps only the current day in `state.last_day` and the last night in `state.last_night`. Paged by the log view; the closing board is `UI.md`'s history, and the board a night opened on (campaign days only) is what its replay is drawn over.
- `realm_game.rs` methods: `try_lock_season_opening`, `open_season`, `next_season`, `live` (the one open or active row), `list_open/active/finished_recent(24h)`, `list_active_for_sweep(now, limit)` (the "is this game behind its own clock" test is in the SQL, from `reset_hour_utc`, so the per-tick bound only lands on games with work to do), `list_finished_unpaid`, `set_payout`, `attach_chat_room` (`ChatRoom::get_or_create_realm_room`; idempotent), `delete_stale_chat_rooms` (30 days after a world ended, voice channels with them; the `DailyMatch` shape), `update_state_cas`, `advance_day_cas`, `finish_cas`, `cancel`, `archive_day`, `load_day_logs`. `REALM_GAME_CHANGED_CHANNEL` is the notify channel the model owns.
- **The snapshot never builds a world.** `game_item` takes the territory total from `map::territory_total` (the spec's own count for a generated map, the static atlas for a fixed one), so re-listing every game after every action is a parse, not a generation. A row whose state no longer parses is left out, logged and counted (`record_realm_snapshot_row_rejected`) the first time it goes missing on this replica, the daily `SnapshotRowError` shape.

## 4. Critical invariants

- An action is resolved exactly once, by the write that wins the row CAS. The retry after a lost CAS re-rolls against the reloaded state (the revision is in the seed); it never replays the old roll.
- A night is resolved exactly once, inside the CAS that advances the day (`close_night` → `write_turn`), and `close_day` is the only thing that turns a day over. `close_night` holds the rule itself: on a row whose open day is not behind the clock it returns without running, whatever its caller believed. A replica that loses the write has run a night nobody will see; it archives and announces nothing.
- The board's odds and the night's come from `night::projection`/`project`, one code path on one board. They differ only by what the viewer's board could not see (others' walls and engagements tonight), and the dispatch says which.
- Orders are secret by presentation: every surface draws the viewer's own orders only, and the standings show a rival's points left on the instant engine only (`RealmState::points_shown`), since on a campaign that number is what they committed tonight. A raw state API, should one appear, must strip other players' orders.
- `Resolution` is matched in three places only (`close_day`, the service entry points, `RealmState`); a fourth is a review finding.
- A *refused* action changes nothing: no point, no log line, no state write. Only rolls are history.
- On the standard ruleset the projected percentage on screen and the rolled percentage come from the same `action_probability` call on the same state. If they ever diverge, the game is lying to players.
- Days are DB integers derived from the game's own `reset_hour_utc` (`svc::realm_day`); never an in-process timer, and never a global midnight.
- The engines (`night.rs`, `resolver.rs`, `map.rs`, `rulesets.rs`) stay pure: no db/tokio imports and no clock; `svc.rs` owns all IO, logging, metrics and spans. The muster freeze is the one rule outside it (`RULES.md`).
- `state.revision` only grows; CAS losers reload.
- Nothing a session is told about another player's write comes from the process that wrote it: `GameChanged`, `DayRolled`, `GameFinished` and `SeasonOpened` are derived from the snapshot diff after a re-read, so they are the same on every replica. A new fan-out event is derived there too, never sent from a mutation.
- At most one world is open or active at any moment, and the table holds that, not the sweeper: `realm_games_one_live`. A season is opened by exactly one replica's tick, the one holding the opening claim; the others spend nothing and read `Standing`.
- The strip's stamp for a moment is the row's own `news.at`, written by the write that made it, never a replica's clock at read time.
- A finished game's pot is settled from the stored row and recorded in `realm_games.payout`; a NULL there means the sweeper still owes it.
- Ruleset snapshots are frozen at the opening; never resolve against the live `RULESETS` entry. New ruleset fields need `#[serde(default)]`.
- Asset format: readers must keep ignoring unknown trailing planes in `earth_grid.bin` so terrain can land later without a version break.
- `LogEntry::Fizzled` and `FizzleReason` carry the campaign's fizzles (`AlreadyYours`, `NoFront`, `TargetProtected`) and the queue era's reasons, which archived days still deserialize through; none may be removed.

## 5. Tests

- **Playouts (`simulation_test.rs`)**: whole games against the real instant engine on the standard ruleset (kept until that engine is deleted), seeded so a failure is reproducible. Two, five and eight players with mixed policies (settler / warlord / cautious / absentee) run to a winner; every day checks the invariants that must never break (land held by the dead, a budget overspent or over its ceiling, decay past its floor, active days exceeding days elapsed, odds outside the ruleset's bounds, an exit without an exit day). Also: the join window staying open at least a week, a latecomer coming out of protection with a position, an absentee surviving but falling behind, two pure turtles never winning, every pace resolving in order, and end-to-end determinism. Observed with the current knobs: **2p resolves in ~47 days, 5p in ~60, 8p in ~79**; the map fills around day 26 (5p) to 37 (8p); the doors to newcomers close around day 8-9. The tests print those numbers (`--nocapture`), so a ruleset change that shifts the feel of the game shows up as a changed line rather than a silent drift.
- Campaign seasons (`night_sim_test.rs`): whole seasons at the house's rules over the real night, every night checked (`RULES.md` "Orders" lists what, and holds the measured table); two quick cases with the suite (five players on Earth leaving a winner with no order refused, the same seed playing the same season), the full scale ignored and run on purpose, every missed bar reported together.
- Orders engine (`night_test.rs`): the named sanity points of the odds, the clamps and monotone odds, a wall raised tonight holding tonight, engagement cutting support, a landing against a march and encirclement, a bigger territory being harder, walls decaying with dormancy; every refusal free and saying why, replace and withdraw refunding, the first order freezing the allowance and keeping the bank; the stronger success taking, an exact tie bouncing, free land tied staying free, a claim on land let go, the fizzles, a fall naming its taker, orders drained; three dominating nights winning, the clock going to the biggest eligible share or dissolving, an equal share going by ranking, the last one standing needing the days; per-target seeds and determinism. A season-one state parsing with every new field defaulted (`resolver_test.rs`).
- Engine (`resolver_test.rs`): points spent per attempt, budgets and refills, refusals for free, the first-come-first-served race, reach and its collapse in the odds, long strikes, an island start sailing instead of being stranded, elimination and last-one-standing, the abandoned-realm dissolve, day rollover, seed determinism (and a retry re-rolling), ranking tie-breaks, the opening truce and its countdown, walls priced as they stand on the day of the roll, the bank never shrinking with a longer absence, a realm short of a rival keeping its doors open.
- Rules (`rulesets.rs` inline): roster lookup, probability clamps and monotonicity, the distance collapse never passing 1%, crossing a rival costing more than going round, a short sea crossing costing less than a march, options as data, a bigger defender beating the linear expectation, tier scaling, snapshot serde.

## 6. Deferred / future hooks

There is no dev key that moves a game through days: a realm pays real chips,
and such a key would mint them. Multi-day rules are exercised in tests
(`svc::act_at`/`sweep` take the clock, and `open_the_doors` backdates a row).

- Delete the instant engine (`apply_action`, `ReachIndex`, the action path in `state.rs`, `svc::act_at`, `Resolution` itself) once the last standard game has ended.
- Animated armies and arrows on the replay; a deterministic fight variant (`force > defence`, an `Orders` sub-option: the roll is one comparison in `resolve_night`, so swapping it is one function).
- Splitting Earth's largest countries into regions (atlas work). Alliances as a mechanic are not planned: the world's chat is the alliance.
- Terrain plane (mountain/forest/desert) in the grid asset; the format already reserves it (`flags` bit0).
- Sea adjacency as a first-class relation (an oversea target is priced from km, which is close enough and needs no asset change), custom/fantasy maps (data-only: new asset pair + `map_by_id` arm + a ruleset), more rulesets (banked points, different decay curves, a no-distance-penalty variant), spectator count, sidebar-panel presence, a voice channel beside the world's chat.
