# Aquarium Context

## Metadata
- Scope: `late-ssh/src/app/hub/aquarium`, plus the care rules in `late-core/src/models/aquarium_care.rs` and the tank-stock transactions in `late-core/src/models/marketplace.rs`.
- Purpose: the Shop-unlocked reef: its simulation, its drawing (Zen tank tile, profile, sidebar Tank panel), and its care (feeding, fry, starvation, sprouts, the shield).
- Parent context: `../CONTEXT.md` (the Shop sells the tank and its stock: `../shop/CONTEXT.md`).

## Files

- `aquarium/`: the animated ambient reef adapted from Reefs; drawn by the Zen tank tile and the profile modal, nowhere else. The sidebar Tank panel is a different drawing of the same population (`ui.rs::draw_mini_tank`, `ui_test.rs`): each creature's required `mini left=".." right=".."` glyph (one row, 1 to 3 cells, `creature.rs::parse_mini`) in a six-row band, placed off the wall tick with no simulation, at most two of a kind and ten in all, hungry fish on the bottom row.

- `state.rs`: embedded aquarium runtime state, per-frame movement, resize binding, and the population (`set_active_creatures`, which carves the fry out of its parent species); `AquariumCare`, the owner's care rules (hunger, murk, the fourteen-box `CareBar`, the visible fry, the shield), all derived from the row, never stored. `state_test.rs`.
- `svc.rs`: `AquariumService`. `bootstrap` at connect: ensures a tank owner's care row, settles starvation (one swimming fish per fourteen unfed days not yet settled, row locked, `marketplace::starve_aquarium_fish_in_tx`), fires the private `AquariumFishLost` events. `feed_task`: `AquariumCare::feed_day` gate + `ChipMove::AquariumFed` (+100) + the fry the streak earns (`hatch_aquarium_fry_in_tx`) in one transaction, then the private `AquariumFed` / `AquariumFryHatched` events (`AquariumFryNoRoom` when the streak came round at the owned cap of fish). Mirrors `BonsaiService::water`. `svc_test.rs`.
- `ui.rs`: top tray and aquarium renderer.
- `config.rs`, `creature.rs`, `world.rs`, `kdl_parse.rs`: embedded KDL config/art parsing and creature/world model.

Tests: `state_test.rs`, `svc_test.rs`, `ui_test.rs`, `creature_test.rs`, and `late-core`'s `aquarium_care_test.rs` / `marketplace_test.rs`. Run them scoped: `make test-llm ARGS="-p late-ssh -E 'test(aquarium)'"`.

## The tank

Aquarium is a Shop unlock, not an admin/mod preview. The Aquarium feature costs 10,000 chips, lives in the Companions Shop category beside its shield, and unlocks Aquarium ownership/use. The tank's stock sits in the same tab under section rows of its own (`CompanionSection` in `../shop/catalog.rs`, which both sorts the tab and labels it: Pet, Bonsai, Aquarium for the unlock and the shield, Growing for the fry and the sprout, Plants, then Fish), browseable before unlock so users can preview them, but purchases and active-count changes are blocked until the Aquarium feature is owned. Fish (`aquarium_fish`) and plants (`aquarium_plant`, migration 183) are the two `TankStockKind`s: bought and parked the same way, capped apart (`AQUARIUM_MAX_FISH`, `AQUARIUM_MAX_PLANTS`, twenty each), and on different clocks: only fish breed and starve, only plants root from sprouts, and plants never die. A tank left for years ends up with no fish and a floor full of plants, which is the point. The reef is drawn on the Zen page (`Ctrl+F`, the tank tile, for everyone: unowned it swims empty under a `/shop` caption) and on the profile, and owners can turn on the sidebar Tank panel, a small stand-in of the same population; there is no Home Lounge tray, no `/aquarium` toggle, and no `show_aquarium_tray` or `show_pet_strip` setting (migration 184 strips both keys from `users.settings`). `/aquarium feed` (or `a` on the Zen page) is the tank's free daily meal: ownership is the only gate, locked users are sent to the Shop modal with a banner, and bare `/aquarium` answers with a usage banner. The sprout is tended on its own Shop row (below), not by a command.

Runtime:
- Fish ownership and active counts persist through `marketplace_items` / `user_purchases`.
- Fish SKUs cost 1,000 chips each and are repeatable purchases; buying the same fish N times gives owned quantity N and does not change active population.
- Twenty fish and twenty plants is the cap on *owning*, in the water and parked together (`TankStockKind::cap`, `aquarium_owned_quantity_in_tx`): a purchase at the cap is refused (`PurchaseStatus::OwnedCapReached`, no chips moved), a fry at the cap is not born (`hatch_aquarium_fry_in_tx` answers `TankSpawn::NoRoom`, the streak still counts, the Fry row says so), and a sprout at the cap withers (`root_aquarium_sprout_in_tx` the same, `SproutFate::Withered`, the `AquariumSproutWithered` event). So an abandoned tank stops at twenty plants exactly, and the inventory never holds more than a tank's worth. The active count is capped by the same number (`adjust_aquarium_active_by_sku`, `aquarium_active_quantity_in_tx`), which only bites for a tank already holding more than twenty (stock bought before migration 183's cap). A bought fish or plant starts in the inventory; `+` puts it in the water.
- `+` / `-` on a fish or plant row adjust its active count, bounded by owned quantity and its kind's cap (`ShopState::adjust_selected_tank_stock`).
- Care events are private (`AquariumFed`, `AquariumFryHatched`, `AquariumFishLost`, `AquariumSprouted`, `AquariumSproutRooted`, `AquariumSproutCut`: never shipped to #lounge); the tank's own session banners them.
- It steps only while the Zen page is up and rebinds on terminal resize; the sidebar Tank panel reads its population and colours but never steps it.
- Active fish and plants are also projected into profile snapshots via `marketplace::active_aquarium_creatures_for_user`; the profile modal paints the reef as a band of its scrolling column (the pet in a column beside it when the user owns both) (`aquarium::ui::draw_into`, into an off-screen buffer) for viewed users with active fish.

Assets live under `late-ssh/assets/aquarium`. The source was adapted from `github.com/mevanlc/reefs`; keep attribution/licensing notes with any future asset or behavior changes. `fry.kdl` is ours: the two-cell hatchling, never sold, coloured like its parent by the population builder. `sprout.kdl` and `seatuft.kdl` (the second plant, kingdom plant, five by three) are ours too.

## Care stakes

Both feeding clocks run on the same fourteen days (`late-core/src/models/aquarium_care.rs`: `CARE_DAYS`, `FRY_DAYS` 7, and the pure rules `dry_days`, `deaths_due`, `hatches_fry`, `fish_weight`, `pick_by_weight`; `aquarium_care_test.rs`). The care row (`user_aquarium_care`, migrations 179 and 180) carries `last_fed`, `streak` (straight fed UTC days, maintained by `feed_day`: plus one when yesterday was fed, else one), `deaths_settled` (starvation deaths taken since the last meal, reset by a meal), `fry_creature` / `fry_born`, and the sprout clock: `sprout_born` (the sprout on the floor, `NULL` for a bare floor) and `next_sprout`.

- **Fry.** The meal that brings the streak to a multiple of fourteen hatches one fry, in the same transaction: a swimming species picked by weight (`fish_weight` = 10,000 / price, times the active count, so a 1,000-chip fish weighs ten tickets a copy and a Bigbert one), `quantity + 1` and `active_quantity + 1`; at twenty owned fish (`AQUARIUM_MAX_FISH`, the same number as the water's cap, so a fry that is born always swims) nothing is born and the `AquariumFryNoRoom` event says so. For its first seven days it swims as the `fry` sprite in a parent colour, taking one of its parent's places in the water; then it is simply one more adult. A fry whose parent species is no longer swimming is not drawn.
- **Starvation.** Settled at connect only (`bootstrap`, like the bonsai's catch-up): unfed days since the last meal, minus the days the shield covered, divided by fourteen, minus `deaths_settled`, each costs one swimming fish picked by the same weight (so a neglected tank loses its cheap fish first and a Bigbert rarely). Quantity and active count both drop; the banner and the private event name the creature. A tank owner who never fed gets a care row at their first connect with `last_fed` set to yesterday, so the clock starts then rather than never. Users without a tank are only read.
- **The welcome fry.** A tank is never bought empty: the purchase transaction also gives one fry (`AQUARIUM_WELCOME_FISH_SKU`, `aquarium_fish_fry`, migration 182: a catalog row whose creature is the hatchling sprite `fry.kdl`) at no price, swimming, and stamps it on the care row as today's fry (`welcome_aquarium_fry_in_tx`, after `AquariumCare::welcome`; `marketplace_test.rs`). It stays the two-cell hatchling: the row's own creature is the fry, so growing up changes nothing on screen. The row exists so the Shop shows the sprite that actually swims: listed first in the Aquarium section with its art and its owned count, never sold: `payload.welcome` (`is_welcome_fish`) makes the purchase path refuse it and the Shop show "with the tank" in place of a price; priced at the small tier so the care rolls weigh it like any small fish, so a streak fry can be another fry and starvation can take it. The rolls are over `swimming_fish_in_tx`, fish only: a fry never grows into a plant. The buying session learns it from the shop snapshot (the one swimming species) and hands it to `welcome_new_tank` with the sprout. It holds one of the twenty places from day one. A tank whose welcome fish predates the fry row (migration 182) holds an MJ, a small fish, instead. The Fry row's detail also says when the next fry hatches (`fed_days_to_next_fry_on`: the straight fed days still needed, by the row's streak rule) and, while a streak fry is small, when it grows into its parent (`fry_growing_on`).
- **Sprouts.** The tank's buds, on a calendar of their own that ignores feeding (`SPROUT_EVERY_DAYS` 14, `SPROUT_DAYS` 7, `sprout_rooted`). The tank comes with its first sprout: the purchase plants the care row with one up today (`AquariumCare::welcome`, inside `purchase_durable_item_by_sku`; the buying session takes the same shape on the next shop snapshot through `welcome_new_tank`, spotted as "the shop says owned, the session's care has no clock"), and migration 180 planted one in every tank that existed at deploy. After that, every fourteen days a sprout comes up on the floor (`sprout_up`: only onto a bare floor, one at a time, and it books the next fourteen days out whatever becomes of this one). It is tended on its own Shop row, `aquarium_sprout` (`AQUARIUM_SPROUT_SKU`, migration 182; an `aquarium_plant` item with `payload.sprout` per migration 183, never a purchase row and refused by the purchase path like the fry): the row shows the sprout's art, its state read off the session's care (`SproutStatus`, `sprout_status_on`: standing with the days left to cut, rooting, or bare with the days to the next one, from `next_sprout`), and `-` on it cuts (`../shop/input.rs` `cut_sprout`, `AquariumService::cut_task`, the row's own gate in `cut_sprout`; the session's `AquariumCare::cut_sprout` applies the same week through `sprout_rooted` and answers `Rooted` past it, so a live session never clears a sprout the row would refuse to cut). Enter and `+` on the row only explain: the tank is tended in one place. A cut sprout is simply gone, no chips either way, and nothing brings it back; the next one comes on its own day. Left alone it roots (`root_sprout`): one of the catalog's plants, any of them as likely as the next (`catalog_plants_in_tx`, `pick_plant_evenly`; the Wigglewort or the Seatuft today), is owned (`root_aquarium_sprout_in_tx`, a zero-price purchase row when it is the first of its kind), in the water when the floor is under the 20-plant cap, else waiting in inventory; at twenty plants owned it withers instead, nothing grows. The plant takes a plant place, never a fish's, and plants never starve: the keep-or-cut is only about how full the floor gets. `SproutFate` (rooted as which creature and whether it swims, or withered) rides `CareBootstrap.rooted`, and the same facts ride `AquariumSproutRooted` / `AquariumSproutWithered`; `svc::sprout_fate_banner` says it. Its Shop row sits in the Companions tab's Growing section, beside the fry. The rooting and the next sprout are settled at connect (the bootstrap) and, for a session up across UTC midnight, on the tick's one-hertz edge (an empty plant catalog fails the settlement loudly, it is a deploy mistake): `take_sprout_settlement_on` asks `settle_sprout_clock_task` once per day when a sprout is past its week or the next is due, and the events do the rest. The sprite is `sprout.kdl` (kingdom plant, three rows, never sold); the session draws it while `AquariumCare::sprout` is set and clears it only on the service's word (a cut on any device, or the rooting), so a long session never shows the sprout gone before the plant arrives. `CareBootstrap.rooted` / `sprouted` carry the connect's news to the first-screen banner, and the same facts ride `AquariumSprouted` / `AquariumSproutRooted` / `AquariumSproutCut` for a second device. The care bar is the only neglect readout.
- **Shield.** `aquarium_shield_two_weeks` (`aquarium_consumable`, effect kind `aquarium_shield`, 2,000 chips, in the Companions tab's Aquarium section, needs the tank): `extend_user_effect_in_tx`, the bonsai shield's stacking shape. A covered day the owner skips counts as neither fed nor unfed: fish are not hungry, the water stays clear, the death clock pauses, and the streak neither grows nor breaks (`streak_continues_from` walks the anchor back over covered days, so the next meal continues it). Feeding during a live shield is an ordinary meal: the chips are paid and the streak advances. The care clocks read *every* window the owner ever bought, live or lapsed (`AquariumShield::all_for_user`, `ShopConsumableEffect::user_effects_for_user`), because a shield that ended last week still excuses the days it covered and a rebuy after a lapse is a second row, not an extension; the session gets the same list at bootstrap and merges the live one from the shop snapshot on purchase (`AquariumCare::refresh_shield`).
- **The bar.** The Zen aquarium tile's title shows fourteen boxes (`CareBar`): fed today, green boxes for the streak (`(streak - 1) % 14 + 1`, so the hatch day is a full bar and the next day one box); not fed, red boxes for the unfed days, saturating at fourteen and staying full until somebody feeds; minded by the shield, all empty.
- **Gotchas.** The streak is UTC days like the rest of care. `feed_day` stamps `last_fed` with the clock whatever `today` it is handed, so the streak rule compares the stored date with `today - 1`; tests that need history write the care row directly (the table under test). Deaths are not settled inside a long-lived session; a tank that crosses fourteen days while its owner stays connected loses the fish on the next connect. The sprout clock also runs on the tick's day edge, so a sprout that passes its week in a live session roots within the second. Both events reach the session through the activity feed, so a second device connecting sees the death banner too; the connecting session also gets it from `AppConfig::initial_aquarium_care.lost`, and the two are the same message, so a double is harmless.
