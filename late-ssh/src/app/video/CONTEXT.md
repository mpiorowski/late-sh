# Video

A YouTube video played inside the user's own terminal through the paired CLI's `mpv` (`late-cli/src/video.rs`, `late-cli/VIDEO.md`); not a byte of media crosses SSH. One player for the whole session, opened from three surfaces: a live stream's room (by itself, or `/watch`; "Live streams" below), a News story (`w` in the News room or the reader, `o` on the live strip or a Live row; `../chat/news/CONTEXT.md`) and a YouTube link in a chat message (Enter or a click on its thumbnail; `../chat/COMMANDS.md`). Held on `App::video`.

## Files

- `state.rs`: pure. `State` (the open `WatchModal`, what the CLI was last told, the `play_id` counter, the session's chat link positions), the `Player` and its `layout`, the `SetupBox` and its `decide`, `sync`, and the closed `WatchSource`, `WatchStatus`, `WatchOutcome`, `WatchMemory`.
- `ui.rs`: draws the player and the setup box over the whole terminal (`app/render.rs`, after the reader).
- `input.rs`: every key and click while the modal is up (`app/input.rs` routes there first).
- `svc.rs`: the gate, opening and closing, where each source's position comes from and goes back to, the sends, the reports, the tick, and the one place a watch is logged and counted.

## Opening

- **What plays** is one rule, `state::youtube_video_id` (`is_watchable` for a yes or no): a YouTube watch, `youtu.be`, embed (no-cookie included), shorts or live link with an 11-character id, the booth's own parse (`audio::youtube::video_id`). A channel or playlist page has no id and is not watchable. News decides its `watch` cards, `w`, the reader and `o` with it; chat decides its thumbnail and Enter with it.
- A caller hands `svc::watch` (or `svc::play`, once it checked `svc::can_play` itself) a `WatchRequest`: the `WatchSource`, the URL mpv gets, a title, who shared it and when. News builds one from the story (`news::State::watch_request`); chat builds one from the selected message (`ChatState::selected_video_in_room`: the canonical `audio::youtube::watch_url` for its id, the oEmbed title cached with its thumbnail for a title, `YouTube video` until it lands or when YouTube names nothing).
- **Both gates** decide player or setup box (`svc::can_play`): the paired CLI advertises `video` (`PairedClientRegistry::has_video_cli`, `ClientAudioState::supports_video`; the CLI sits in the terminal and checked it, tmux included, and WezTerm reads as iTerm2 to the server's image detection, so that detection is no gate) and the Tweaks "Terminal images" row is not `Off`. `svc::watch` returns the closed `Watched` (`Player` / `Setup`) so News marks a story read only when it opened.
- Opening closes whatever modal was up first (`svc::close`), so its position is kept. Nothing else on the page gets a key or a click while it is up, so the News reader and the player never coexist: the reader's `w` closes the reader after handing the story over. The page-change and modal-opening glue closes both through one root helper, `app/input.rs::close_reader_and_player`.

## The player

- A ` Watching ` box (90 percent of the terminal, width capped at 160, title and `shared by @mat 2h ago` on one header row, a rule, a footer) or, on `f`, the whole terminal but the footer row (`Player::layout`, pure). Its body is painted black and is the rectangle the CLI gets.
- The modal is pure state: after every draw `svc::sync` asks `State::cli_messages` (the pure `sync` over what the CLI was last told, `LivePlay`, held on `State` past the modal) for the messages that bring the CLI in line, so opening sends `video_play`, `f` or a resize `video_place`, and anything that makes the player go away (Esc, `[x]`, a page change, another modal, the session's `Drop`) `video_stop`, all through one comparison.
- Keys relay `video_control` (`Space` pause, `←`/`→` or `h`/`l` seek 10 s, `Shift+←`/`→` or `H`/`L` a minute, `0` or Home back to the top, `m` mpv's own mute); `c` copies the link; `f` toggles the size.
- `play_id` is a per-session counter on `State`; every send goes through `send_control_to_video_cli`, and one that reaches nobody turns the player `Lost`. The CLI's `video_state` arrives as `SessionMessage::VideoState` (`api.rs`, routed by token like `clipboard_image`, not an `ArticleEvent`, since it belongs to one session, not every session of the user) and `svc::apply_report` updates the player only when its `play_id` matches.
- The footer status reads `starting…`, `▶ 1:23 / 12:04`, `⏸ …`, `ended`, `couldn't play`, or `lost the CLI` (no report for 10 s while playing or paused, `WATCH_LOST_AFTER`, checked on the world tick; a starting play is exempt, since the CLI says nothing between its one `starting` and mpv's first position and a yt-dlp resolve can outlast the timeout; `Lost` is final, the stop already went out, so a late report never replays); the last two show `Watch on the web: <url>` in the body, and a failure raises `Couldn't play the video.` once.
- A video outlasts the idle limit without a key, so the screensaver waits while the video has the screen (`App::screensaver_due`, `State::on_screen`: the modal, or an inline stream the last frame drew). A stream only heard has no picture to fight and does not hold it back.

## Live streams

- A live stream room plays its stream by itself. `svc::auto_watch(app, drawn)` runs every tick from `App::tick_stream` with the room whose live stream row the chat pane drew last frame (`ChatState::last_stream_room_drawn`, set by `chat::ui::draw_room_header`), and starts that room's stream when the session's switch is on (`State::may_auto_start`), no modal is up (`App::foreground_overlay_open`: a video, the setup box, help), the stream is not already open here, and the session can play one. A different stream that was playing gives way to it. An attempt that opens nothing (the stream just ended, no ticket, the viewer is the streamer) is remembered for that room and not repeated every tick (`State::decline_auto`); entering the room again asks again. A streamer is never played their own stream unasked: it would be their own sound, late.
- A bare `/watch` is the switch (`svc::toggle_stream`, `StreamSwitch`, on by default, kept for the session). While a stream plays, seen or only heard, from any room: it stops it and turns the switch off, and no stream room plays by itself until `/watch` is typed again. With nothing playing, in a live stream's room: it turns the switch on and plays that stream now. Anywhere else it is a usage error. There is no button; `/watch` is the one way to stop a stream, and the stream row of the room's header says what it would do now (`svc::switch_hint`: `/watch to stop`, `/watch to play`, or nothing for a session that cannot play one).
- The stream is received by the paired CLI itself: `StreamService::cli_watch` gives the watch page's subscribe-only LiveKit ticket, and the play goes out as `video_play_stream` instead of `video_play` (`WantedMedia::Stream`, `late-cli/VIDEO.md`). The source is `WatchSource::LiveStream(LiveTicket)`; the ticket's `Debug` leaves the token out, since every watch log line prints its source.
- The gate is `can_play_stream`: a paired CLI advertising `video_stream` (`PairedClientRegistry::has_video_stream_cli`; everything `video` needs but yt-dlp) and "Terminal images" not `Off`. A session that fails it and typed `/watch` gets one banner, `Can't play streams in this terminal. Watch in your browser with /watch @user.`, and no modal: no setup box, no browser fallback. It plays here or it does not. The automatic start says nothing at all.
- A stream plays inline, in its room's pane, not over the page: the room's chat is half of watching one, so nothing may take the keys. A player has one size, `WatchSize` (`Small` or `Full`), and opens small: for a video that is the window, for a `LiveStream` its room's pane. While a stream is small `State::modal()` is `None` (input, the draw of the modal, and `App::foreground_overlay_open` all read that), with `State::inline_slot()` offered to the chat pane instead. `chat::ui::draw_room_header` seats it under the header rows (`draw_inline_stream`): a black rectangle as wide as the pane, 16:9 where that fits, at most three fifths of the pane and never leaving the messages under 6 rows (`inline_stream_rows`), with a rule under it. The pane reports the rectangle back through `InlineSlot::place`, and that is the body `sync` sends the CLI, so a resize or a moved pane becomes a `video_place` like any other. The rectangle carries `InlineSlot::caption` while it has no picture (`connecting…`, or the failed play's `Watch on the web:` line).
- A stream outlives its picture. `State::begin_frame` (top of `App::render`) clears the inline placement, and a playing stream no pane placed that frame wants `Seat::Hidden`: `sync` sends `video_hide`, the CLI takes the picture down and keeps the stream, and its sound follows the user into other rooms and pages (the music case). That covers another room, another page, a pane too short for a picture, and a modal over the room (`render.rs` withholds the slot while `foreground_overlay_open`, since the CLI's picture would sit on top of the modal). Drawn again, it wants `Seat::At(rect)` and `sync` sends a `video_place`, which the CLI answers with a fresh picture. A play starts only once it has somewhere to draw (`sync` sends nothing for a first `Hidden`). A stream that stopped playing (failed, lost) and is not on screen is closed by `svc::sync` (`State::unseen_dead_stream`): nothing plays and nobody is reading why.
- The room rail marks the playing stream's row `♪` instead of `▶` (`State::playing_stream_room` through `ChatRoomListView.stream_playing`), since the sound may come from a room the user is not in.
- A click on the picture takes it full screen (`input::click_inline_stream`, the one call the root click handler makes; `Player::toggle_size`). Full screen is the `Full` layout of any video and a modal again: it takes the keys, `m` mutes, and `f`, Esc and `[x]` all go back to the room with the stream still playing (`svc::dismiss`, `Player::back_inline`). A page change or another modal opening dismisses it the same way (`input::close_reader_and_player`). There is no mute key inline: the composer owns the keys there.
- Live means no pause, seek or resume: `Player::control` lets only `ToggleMute` out, the full-screen footer offers `m` and `f` (`back to chat`), the status reads `connecting…`, `● live`, `stream ended`, `couldn't play` or `lost the CLI` (the CLI's position counts frames and is never shown), and `Player::memory` is `Nothing`. The full-screen header's meta is `@streamer · live`.
- While the player is up `svc::tick` calls `StreamService::watch_heartbeat` once a second under the ticket's `stream_id` and `watcher_id` (`cli-{session}`): that keeps this viewer in the stream's watcher count, and a `false` (the stream left the registry) ends the play (`Player::end`, counted as `ended`), closes the player, inline or full screen, and leaves the banner `The stream ended.`; the next draw's `sync` sends the stop. The same second reads `StreamService::cli_voices` with the viewer's voice channel: when they joined or left the stream's channel since the play began, `Player::set_voices` updates the ticket and a `video_voices` goes out, so the room's voices are never heard twice (the CLI's voice runtime and the stream) or dropped. A failed or lost play stays up and shows `Watch on the web: <watch page>`, as a video does.

## Resuming

- A player is told to start `WATCH_RESUME_REWIND_S` (3 s) before where its video was left (`video_play.start_s`, mpv's `--start`; zero for a fresh play), reads `resuming from 23:14…` while it starts and `▶ 23:11 / 48:30 · resumed` for `WATCH_RESUME_NOTE_FOR` (4 s) once it plays.
- What a closing player leaves is one pure decision, `Player::memory` (`WatchMemory`): the last reported position while partway through (`Save`, a failed or lost play included, since `last_seen` outlives the status); a clear once the play `Ended`, stopped inside the last `WATCH_SAVE_END_MARGIN_S` (30 s), or was taken back under `WATCH_SAVE_MIN_S` (10 s) with a position stored (the one it resumed from, or a checkpoint since); nothing when no position ever came in or a play with nothing stored stopped inside its first ten seconds.
- Where it comes from and goes back to is one `match` on `WatchSource` in `svc.rs` each way (`svc::play`, `svc::close`): a `NewsArticle` reads and writes the reader's `article_reads` row through News (`news::State::watch_position`, `remember_watch`, `save_watch_position`; `../chat/news/CONTEXT.md`, Resuming), so it resumes on any machine; a `ChatLink` has no row and lives on `State::link_positions`, keyed by URL, for the session only.
- While a News story plays, `svc::apply_report` asks `Player::checkpoint` after every report, which saves the position every `WATCH_SAVE_EVERY` (15 s) past the first ten seconds while playing or paused, so a dropped session or a dead CLI still resumes close to where it was; a play that is over is the close's to decide, so no final report writes a position the close then clears. A chat link's checkpoint is dropped: the close keeps it, and a dropped session loses it anyway.

## The setup box

` Watch in the terminal `, width capped at 72, lists what a play needs and marks each row `✓` holds, `✗` fails, or `·` unknown, all decided in one pure `SetupBox::decide` over `SetupInputs` (the paired CLI's last `client_state` via `PairedClientRegistry::cli_state`, the protocol, a tmux/screen `TERM`, a Windows Terminal env hint, the Tweaks row): `the late CLI` (✗ with no paired CLI; `late in native mode` ✗ for a paired CLI in OpenSSH or an old mode; a CLI reporting neither `video` nor any `video_blockers` predates video and reads `an up-to-date late CLI`), `a Kitty-graphics terminal` (once a CLI reports, its word: ✓ unless it sent one of `not_kitty`, `no_pixel_size`, `platform`; before that the server's detection only confirms: ✓ on Kitty, `·` otherwise), `no tmux or screen`, `mpv 0.36 or newer` (`mpv is too old` for `mpv_too_old`), `yt-dlp` (both `·` until a CLI that knows about video reports). Tweaks images `Off` leads with `terminal images on`. One install line follows: the CLI installer (`irm …` on Windows Terminal) without a reporting CLI, otherwise by the CLI's platform when a tool is missing (package manager, `brew install mpv yt-dlp`, `Windows is not supported yet`); then `Watch on the web: <url>`. `c` copies, Esc or `[x]` closes.

## Telemetry

`metrics::record_video_watch` labels `late_ssh_video_watches_total` by `source` (`news`, `chat`, `stream`, from `WatchSource`) and the closed `WatchOutcome` (`opened`, `setup`, `played`, `ended`, `failed`, `lost`). A failure is logged with the CLI's reason (`video_watch_failed`, never shown), a loss with its cause (`video_watch_lost`), the rest at `info`; every line carries the source.
