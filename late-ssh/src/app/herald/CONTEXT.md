# The herald (`app/herald`) Context

## Metadata
- Domain: late.sh's social accounts. Two posts a day, posted by the server through an ordinary account on each network it is on (Mastodon, Bluesky), so the things members make to be seen (the gallery's pieces, the Artboard, their projects) and the rituals they share (the week's games, the daily paper) are seen outside the terminal.
- Status: Active, live. Both accounts post from prod: `@late@fosstodon.org` and `late.sh` on Bluesky. Prod pins both; the `herald_settings` row is on, and the operator flips it (below).

## What it is

An arrivals lever built to the shape in `AUDIENCE.md` ("The herald"): post, do not federate. One account per network, a token each, a few HTTP calls a day. The only session side is the admin's `/herald`; nothing a member does triggers a post. It reads what members already made public (the shared board, the pieces they hung, the projects they shared, the public boards and the paper) and never members' chat: the one room it reads is the operator's `#announcements`. A post is picked, worded and fetched once, then delivered to every configured network with the same words and the same picture, so a change to what it says or shows changes it everywhere. Two slots, each its own claim and its own hour: the **words** (the operator's word, a project, the paper, the week's scoreboard) and the **art** (a gallery piece or the Artboard). A slot never posts the other's kinds, and art goes out clean or not at all.

## Data

- `herald_posts` (model `late-core/src/models/herald.rs`, migrations 252 to 257, 263 and 275): one row per slot of a UTC day (`day`, `slot`), the claim. `id` is minted at the first claim and never changes: Mastodon sees it as the `Idempotency-Key`, Bluesky as the record key derived from it, so the day's two posts never share a key. `posting` is held while the calls run; `kind` (`artboard`, `piece`, `scoreboard`, `paper`, `announcement`, `project`), `subject` (the board's `daily:YYYY-MM-DD` key, `piece:{id}`, `week:{last day}`, `paper:{edition}`, `announcement:{edition}`, `project:{id}`), `picture` (the serialized `Picture`) and `look` (the serialized `Look`) are written at pick time in one statement (`store_pick`), before the first call, so a retry delivers the same post with the same alt text and never asks the model again; a check holds `picture` and `look` both or neither; `posted` means every configured network delivered; `skipped` is a slot with nothing to post or nothing clean; `failed` keeps `attempts`. Claim semantics as `job_press_runs` and the paper's rows, plus a window: a stale `posting` (older than `HERALD_STALE_CLAIM`, 20 minutes) is taken over and a `failed` row is claimed again, both under `HERALD_MAX_ATTEMPTS` (3) and only while the row's first claim (`first_claimed_at`) is younger than `HERALD_CLAIM_WINDOW` (50 minutes); past the window a stale `posting` rests as `failed`, and the slot is finished either way. The project rows are also the cooldown's memory (`picked_since`), and a pick counts whether or not it settled, since a failed post may already be on one network. Migration 275 keyed the table on the slot, filed each old row under its kind's slot, and settled the other slot of the days the checker could still owe, so the first check after it never catches up beside a post already out.
- `herald_deliveries`: one row per network a post landed on (`day`, `slot`, `network`), written as each call succeeds; a retry sends only to the networks without one.
- `herald_settings` (`HeraldSwitch` in the same model): the operator's switch, one row keyed `posting`, read at every check, seeded off by migration 252. Herald-owned like `greendragon_settings`; the process-wide `app_flags` table was dropped in migration 219 and stays dropped.
- Reads of other domains, through model surfaces only: the board's key is `Snapshot::daily_key` (the rollover writes under the same call) and the board comes from `Snapshot::find_by_board_key` and is decoded with `dartboard_core::Canvas` to count and locate its ink; the piece from `ArtboardPiece::splash_for_day` (the splash wall's piece for the day, `app/artboard/gallery/CONTEXT.md`: one queue in hang order, a piece a day, the day after it was hung at the earliest; the herald claims through the wall's own claim when the gallery's hourly refresh has not yet, so the post and the login screen show one piece, and nothing in the herald remembers which pieces went out); the projects from `Showcase::random_active` (random order, the author logged in within `PROJECT_ACTIVE_WINDOW`, 7 days, by `users.last_seen`, which is stamped at connect, and not banned; the cooldown's ids left out); the week from `leaderboard::week_tallies` (the arcade's daily wins and score events, the Lobby's `daily_matches`, the doors' `door_openings`); the edition from `PaperEdition::load` (a `ready` YESTERDAY ON LATE.SH, WHAT WE WERE READING or OUTSIDE column, the ones the web prints; the candidate carries the numbers whole and the heads of the other two, for the alt text) and the covered day's most played arcade game from `leaderboard::day_tallies` and `DayTally::most_played_arcade`, the rule late-web's card draws its headline with, so the alt text reads the card in its order: the headline, the numbers, reading, outside; the announcements the same way the paper reads them (`app/paper`): once the edition `is_swept`, `ChatRoom::find_public_non_dm_by_slug` for `#announcements` and `ChatMessage::list_public_room_between_with_author` over `PaperEdition::window`, the bodies joined, as written.

## The post

- **When.** Every replica checks each slot every `HERALD_CHECK_INTERVAL` (5 minutes). The words are due at `WORDS_POST_TIME`, 15:00 UTC (late afternoon in Europe, the morning on the US east coast, ahead of the room's busiest hour, 18:00 to 19:00 UTC, so whoever clicks through finds people in), the art at `ART_POST_TIME`, 20:00 UTC (the evening in Europe, the afternoon in the US), five hours apart so the two never stack in one timeline. No study settles these hours for either network; the room's own profile (`AUDIENCE.md`) is the one measured input, and each is one constant. `post_due_day` names the day a slot owes, so a replica that was down at the hour catches up and stamps the right day. A settled slot is memoized per replica, so the rest of the day costs one flag read per check and nothing else.
- **What: the words.** The operator's word first: an edition whose covered day has `#announcements` posts makes the slot an announcement, whatever the weekday, so a release note posted on Monday goes out Tuesday at 15:00. Else by `words_schedule`: a project on Monday and, while one is still owed, through to Friday; the paper on Wednesday and Saturday, the Late Edition dated the day when a public column printed (its card leads with the arcade's most played game, `late-web/CONTEXT.md`), and on Monday when no project could run; on Sunday the week to Saturday in every game (`week_tallies`: per game everyone's plays added up, the daily solves, the arcade runs, the finished Lobby matches, the door openings; totals, and nobody named: the card is public, nobody opted into being posted, and the names stay on the terminal's boards), else the paper. Sunday and not Saturday because the week closes at the end of Saturday. A slot whose kinds all have nothing is `skipped`: settled, not retried, and on Tuesday, Thursday and Friday with nothing owed and nothing announced that is the plan: the art still goes out.
- **What: the art.** One candidate a day and no fallback (`art_day`): on Monday yesterday's daily Artboard snapshot once it has `BOARD_MIN_INK` (200) drawn cells (the board is wiped on the first of the month, and the days after it are a few strokes); every other day the piece over the door (the splash wall's piece for the day, above). A piece with no day, a bare board, or a look that is not clean rests the slot for the day. A flagged piece does not reach for the board, since one nude figure stays on the board until the wipe and a flagged piece's board is nearly always flagged too. The wall moves on every day whatever the herald posts, so Monday's piece hangs over the door and is not posted, and neither is one the look flagged; a piece is never posted twice because no day has two pieces. The poster kind of the first design was dropped before launch: too few new posters to carry a slot.
- **What: a project.** One a week, drawn at random from the projects (`showcases`, `app/directory`) of members active in the last 7 days, none posted in the last 60 (`PROJECT_COOLDOWN`), and each one the post can carry: a link that is http or https with a real host (`ProjectFound::of`), and room for at least `PROJECT_MIN_WORDS` (60) characters of words after the title, the credit and the link. Up to `PROJECT_TRIES` (3) are put to the model, one at a time. A refusal (`commercial`, since Fosstodon bans commercial promotion; `unfit`; `unclear`) moves to the next; all refused, or AI off, or a failed call, is no project today: the slot's next kind goes and the week still owes one, so the next day to Friday draws again. A picked project pays the week (`project_exclusions`, Monday on). A member's own words never go out unchecked, unlike an announcement's, since nobody who runs the account wrote them.
- **Where the picture comes from.** late-web's PNG routes (`late-web/CONTEXT.md`), fetched once per attempt over `HeraldConfig::web_internal_url` (the cluster service in prod, the compose service in dev): the board at `/gallery/{key}/image.png?crop=x,y,w,h`, cropped to its drawn cells plus `CROP_MARGIN` and rendered at the crop's own scale, so a sparse board is a few strokes, not a dark field; a piece at `/gallery/pieces/{id}/image.png`; the scoreboard at `/games/week/{last}/image.png` and the paper at `/paper/{edition}/image.png` (the numbers column first, then the heads of the prose columns), both text cards late-web draws from the same rows. One render a day on service-web's CPU.
- **The brief.** At pick time, an announcement's words are one text call with the note as written: the model is asked for two or three short paragraphs under `ANNOUNCEMENT_MAX_CHARS` (225) that lead with the change a reader cares about, keep every command, key, path and name exactly as the note writes them, state nothing the note does not, name nobody, carry no markdown, tags, links or mentions (the page is added after), and sound like a person telling a friend what changed: everyday words, no launch voice, no exclamation marks, the marketing vocabulary named and banned in `brief.rs`. The answer is `Brief::Model`; AI off or a failed call is `Brief::Cut`, the note as written. Either way the words pass `plain_lines` (markdown markers dropped, paragraph breaks kept), `no_dashes` (an em or en dash made a comma) and `dead_words` and are cut at the limit with an ellipsis, and the post goes out: the brief never skips a post. Stored on the row with the pick, so a retry posts the same words. The look is `Drawn` (there is no picture): the words alone on both networks, as short paragraphs, then `Full notes: ` and the edition's page, then `#tildeverse #terminal #TUI`. Mastodon draws its own preview card from the link; Bluesky draws none for a post made through the API, so the record carries one (`app.bsky.embed.external`: the edition's page, `The Late Edition, {date}` and the edition's first OUTSIDE or READING line as the web's own card reads it, `AnnouncementCandidate::link_card`), its thumb the site's skyline fetched from late-web at `state::SITE_IMAGE_PATH` (`/static/og-image.png`, the one path late-web's `meta.rs` names) per attempt like a picture.
- **The write-up.** At pick time, a project's words are one grounded call with its title, link, description and tags, marked as untrusted data: instructions in a member's text or on their page are not followed. The model is asked for one or two plain sentences on what the project does, from the description and the project's own page only (a different thing with the same name is ignored), naming nobody, with no markdown, tags, links or mentions and the brief's plain voice. The words pass the same `plain_lines`, `no_dashes` and `dead_words` and are cut at `ProjectFound::words_limit`, what is left of `PROJECT_POST_MAX_CHARS` (295) after the title (cut at 60, dead), the credit, the link and `#tildeverse #terminal`. The post reads `From the late.sh clubhouse: "title" by ~name.`, the words, the member's link, the tags. The look is `Drawn`: words alone, with the same link card on Bluesky (the member's link, the title, the words, the skyline as its thumb). Mastodon draws its preview card from the member's own page, which the look never sees. Stored on the row with the pick, so a retry posts the same words.
- **The look.** At pick time, after the fetch and before the store, the picture goes to the model whole and in four quarters (the whole board reaches it shrunk, and only the quarters showed it the small doodles in the hand run) with a schema answer: a description (`alt`), one warning from the closed set (`none`, `nudity`, `suggestive`, `explicit`, `gore`, `offensive`) and the piece that set it (`reason`, for the operator: the `/herald preview` banner and the log line). The prompt in `look.rs` labels what is drawn, never small text or jokes in words (a slur drawn large is the one exception), never identity (a pride flag, the word gay), and counts a drawn penis, even a doodle, as explicit. A piece gets the same rules with its own paragraph (one member's titled work, not a collage). The look is the gate: `Look::goes_out` is true only for a clean look or a card, and `pick_for` is the one place that reads it. Fosstodon bans adult and violent content even behind a content warning, so anything flagged stays off every network, and so does `Unseen` (a failed call or AI off): the slot settles `skipped`, nothing is stored, and the line `the herald left a picture unposted` says why. A stored pick from before this rule rests the same way. The cards are `Drawn`: late.sh's own words, clean by construction, the alt text written from the same rows, no call. One call on an art day (plus previews), about 6,000 input tokens and up to 16,384 out, most of it thinking.
- **The words of a post.** One text, under 300 graphemes (Bluesky's limit, the tightest; Mastodon allows 500): a line of plain speech, the page (the gallery for the board, not the day's snapshot, which the Artboard keeps for seven days; the piece's page; the edition, written `Full edition: ` for the paper and `Full notes: ` for an announcement so the link reads as what it is; the front page for the scoreboard, whose boards live in the terminal; the member's own link for a project), the kind's tags (the board and pieces `#ASCIIart #MastoArt #tildeverse #terminal`, the scoreboard `#puzzles #sudoku #tildeverse #terminal`, the paper `#tildeverse #terminal #TUI #smolweb`, an announcement `#tildeverse #terminal #TUI`, a project `#tildeverse #terminal`). A member a kind credits is `~name`, never `@name`, since a bare `@` is a mention of whoever has that name on the instance; nothing else names anyone. A piece's or a project's title is member-typed, and an announcement's or a project's words are the operator's or the model's, so they go out dead (`state::dead_words`): no `@` or `#`, and a space after every colon that closes a word so no URL scheme survives (a colon after a digit stays, so a time reads `16:00`), or the official account would mention, tag or link whatever they say. The alt text keeps a piece's title as typed; no network reads mentions or links there. The same alt text on every network: `ASCII art: ` and the look's description for the board and pieces, the card's own words for the cards.
- **Mastodon.** A picture: `POST /api/v2/media` as multipart (`file`, `description`), 200 with the attachment or 202 polled on `GET /api/v1/media/{id}` until 200; then `POST /api/v1/statuses` with `status`, `media_ids`, `visibility` (`MastodonConfig::visibility`: public in prod, unlisted in dev), `language: en` and the `Idempotency-Key` header. Words alone: the status call alone, `media_ids` empty. The status's `url` is the delivery's.
- **Bluesky.** `com.atproto.server.createSession` with the handle and the app password, `com.atproto.repo.uploadBlob` with the PNG (shrunk by 20% a side per pass until under `BLOB_MAX_BYTES`, 950,000, the PDS's cap being a megabyte), then `com.atproto.repo.putRecord` of an `app.bsky.feed.post` with the text, `langs`, the embed (the picture with its alt and aspect ratio, or a link card with its thumb) and facets (`#link` for the page, `#tag` per hashtag, by UTF-8 byte range). The record key is a TID built from the row's UUID v7 (its millisecond timestamp and ten bits of its randomness), so the same post puts the same record. The delivery's URL is `https://bsky.app/profile/{handle}/post/{rkey}`. Bluesky has no unlisted post: a dev post there is public.
- **Never.** Chat, a frame of #lounge, anything a member did not make to be seen. A flagged picture on any network, a member's project unchecked by the model, a third post a day. Removal of a post already out is a hand job from the account.

## Config and switches

- `Config::herald` (`HeraldConfig` in `late-ssh/src/config.rs`): `mastodon: Option<MastodonConfig>` (instance, token, visibility), `bluesky: Option<BlueskyConfig>` (pds, identifier, app password), `web_internal_url`. A network configured is the herald on (`enabled()`); none is off. Prod pins `https://fosstodon.org` and the Bluesky handle `late.sh` as literals and requires `LATE_HERALD_MASTODON_TOKEN` and `LATE_HERALD_BLUESKY_PASSWORD`, injected from the `herald-credentials` secret (`infra/secrets.tf`, `infra/service-ssh.tf`; GitHub secrets `HERALD_MASTODON_TOKEN` and `HERALD_BLUESKY_PASSWORD` through both terraform workflows). A new network is the same shape: pin its literal, `required` its secret, add the variable, the secret key, the env and the two workflow lines, shipped as an `-infra` release before the ssh image that needs it (`infra/RUNBOOK.md`, deploy skew rule). Dev: each network on only with its pair in `.env.local` (`LATE_HERALD_MASTODON_INSTANCE` + `..._TOKEN`, `LATE_HERALD_BLUESKY_IDENTIFIER` + `..._PASSWORD`; half a pair refuses to start), `web_internal_url` the compose web service, Mastodon unlisted. `Config::validate` refuses a configured network with an empty credential or a non-HTTP URL.
- The row: `/herald on` and `/herald off` in chat, admins only (the same statement through `scripts/connect_db.sh` works too); `off` stops the next check, no deploy. `on` first settles what the switch slept through, per slot: before a slot's hour its due day is yesterday, and with no row that post is written `skipped` (zero attempts) before the row flips, so the first post after enabling is the next hour and never a catch-up of a two-day-old board; after the hour the due day is today and, unposted, it goes out at the next check. The banner says which, for each slot. The config is the deployment's switch; the row is the operator's.
- `/herald status` reads the row and today's two slots (no post yet, posting, posted where, skipped, failed after how many attempts). `/herald preview words|art` shows what that slot's post would be today (the pick, by subject; on a piece day the piece over the door, so a login shows the same picture) and to which networks, and runs a fresh look at it (clean, or the warning and its reason and that it would stay unposted, with the alt text in the banner, which a long alt overflows), a fresh brief or a fresh write-up (the words), sending nothing; with nothing to post it says what the slot lacked. `/herald post words|art` works that slot of today now: it claims the row like the checker does, so a post goes out once however many admins type it, and the slot's check finds it settled. It ignores the row on purpose (an admin asking is the switch for that post) but not the config: with no network configured it says so. Neither has a default: there are two posts a day, so bare `preview` and `post` are the usage banner. Every subcommand is refused with a banner for non-admins; the answer is one banner line (`svc::banner_for`), sent back over the service's channel to the session that asked.
- Account setup is by hand: Mastodon's bot flag and profile links in its web settings, the application with `write:media` and `write:statuses` under Development; Bluesky's app password under Settings, Privacy and security, and its automation label under Settings, "Show automation label" (the `bot` self-label on the profile record). The landing page on late-web links both accounts with `rel="me"`, which is what the Mastodon profile's verified check reads.

## Telemetry

- `late_ssh_herald_posts_total{result}`: `posted`, `skipped`, `lost` (a check that found the slot held by another replica; a held slot is not memoized, so it counts per check until the holder settles it), `failed` (the post, or the claim itself, failed). `late_ssh_herald_deliveries_total{network,result}`: `delivered`, `failed` per leg. `late_ssh_herald_looks_total{result}`: `clean`, `warned` and `unseen` (both stay unposted), `drawn` (a card, no call), per look run (a fresh pick or a preview; a retry reuses the stored look and counts nothing). `late_ssh_herald_briefs_total{result}`: `model`, `as_written` (AI off), `failed`, per brief run, the same way. `late_ssh_herald_projects_total{result}`: `written`, `refused`, `ai_off`, `failed`, per project put to the write-up. Grafana "Herald" row, `monitoring/dashboards/observability.json`.
- Spans `herald.check` around every check, with its slot, and `herald.command` around an admin's command. Error types `herald_flag_failed`, `herald_claim_failed`, `herald_delivery_failed` (per network, once, with the network), `herald_post_failed` (the post's summary), `herald_command_failed` (a command's read or flip failed), `herald_look_failed` (the model's error, once; the picture stays unposted), `herald_brief_failed` (the model's error, once; the post goes out as written, cut), `herald_writeup_failed` (the model's error, once; no project this time); info lines `the herald delivered` (day, slot, network, url), `the herald posted` (day, slot, kind, subject, look, urls), `the herald left a picture unposted` (day, slot, subject, what the look said), `the herald's write-up turned a project down` (project, refusal, reason) and `the herald had nothing worth posting`; a warn when a failed post's row cannot be marked.

## Gotchas

- **Two idempotency mechanisms, one key, one window.** Mastodon keeps an `Idempotency-Key` for about an hour, so every retry of a post has to fall inside it: a slot is never claimed again once its first claim is older than `HERALD_CLAIM_WINDOW` (50 minutes), whatever `attempts` says, and a stale `posting` row past the window rests as `failed` for the operator. Inside the window a lost answer is retried under the same key (stale after 20 minutes, three claims at most) and Mastodon answers with the status it already made; Bluesky's `putRecord` under the same TID is an upsert at any time. A delivery that landed but could not be recorded is sent again under the same key, which both networks dedupe. So a lost answer never double-posts; a post that dies past the window is a hand job, never a retry.
- **The picture is fetched per attempt, not per network**, and the pick is stored before the first call: a retry after a partial failure shows the same picture on the network that missed it.
- **The look is one reading, not a verdict.** A drawing the look misses ships, and the herald's copy on a network is removed by hand from the account, as is a piece's post when the piece comes down (its web page 404s at once). The model misses things: in the hand run over the archived boards it named a poster wrongly, and only the quarters made it see a small drawn penis. Its labels vary between runs on the same board (nudity one run, suggestive the next).
- **Most boards this month carry a warning.** The live board keeps its drawings until the monthly wipe, so one nude figure flags every daily until the first of the month, and every Monday in that stretch posts no art. That is why the board has one day a week and no fallback role. Fosstodon's rules (`/api/v1/instance/rules` and its code of conduct at `hub.fosstodon.org/coc`): "DO NOT post adult or age-restricted content. This includes nudity and other sexualized or violent content", with no exception for a content warning, plus no slurs, alt text on images, no commercial promotion through link-only or hashtag-heavy posts, and automated accounts monitored by a member. Pieces are mostly clean, the cards always are.
- **The brief's and the write-up's words go out unread.** They are generated at pick time, so `/herald preview` shows a sample run, not the text that posts (a project preview also draws at random, so it may not even be the same project). The prompts hold the model to their sources and to no new claims, `dead_words` keeps it from tagging or mentioning anyone, and a wrong word is removed by hand from the account. The write-up names a real member's work, so a wrong word there lands on them. The one way to approve the exact words is to post them by hand instead.
- **`/herald post` posts today, the checker posts the due day.** Before a slot's hour the checker owes yesterday and the admin's command works today, so a hand post in the morning and the checker's catch-up of yesterday can both happen on one day; after the hour they are the same day and the row keeps it to one post.
- **A long outage stacks the two posts.** A replica back after both hours works both slots at its next check, back to back. Neither is retried later, and both are correct for the day.
- **Deploying the slot migration.** An old replica's claim fails against the new key (`herald_claim_failed`, no post) until it is replaced, which is harmless and clears when the rollout does.
- **The human half is operator work.** Answer replies, boost members, follow people (`AUDIENCE.md`). Fosstodon's rule for automated accounts is that an active member runs them; Bluesky's is that a bot never interacts unprompted, so boosting members there is done from a person's own account.
