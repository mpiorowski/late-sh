# The herald (`app/herald`) Context

## Metadata
- Domain: late.sh's social accounts. One picture a day, posted by the server through an ordinary account on each network it is on (Mastodon, Bluesky), so the things members make to be seen (the Artboard first; more kinds in `AUDIENCE.md`, the herald's future work) are seen outside the terminal.
- Status: Active, shipped dark. Both accounts exist and have taken a post from a dev stack: `@late@fosstodon.org` and `late.sh` on Bluesky. Prod pins both; the `herald_settings` row starts off and the operator flips it (below).

## What it is

An arrivals lever built to the shape in `AUDIENCE.md` ("The herald"): post, do not federate. One account per network, a token each, a few HTTP calls a day. The only session side is the admin's `/herald`; nothing a member does triggers a post. It reads what members already made public (the shared board) and never chat. One post is picked, worded and fetched once, then delivered to every configured network, so a change to what it says or shows changes it everywhere.

## Module map

| File | Role |
|---|---|
| `svc.rs` | `HeraldService`: the checker on every replica (`start_task`, `check`), `work_day` (the claim, the stored pick, the picture fetch, one delivery per network, the settle), and the admin's commands (`request` runs `run_command` off the session and answers over a broadcast channel; `tick` drains the answer into a banner and hands `/herald` from the composer to the service); every log line, metric and error type. |
| `state.rs` | Pure rules: `post_due_day` and `HERALD_POST_TIME` (16:00 UTC), `shown_day` and `board_key`, `ink_cells` against `BOARD_MIN_INK`, `BoardLook` (the board as found, its ink, `picture` at the ink bar), `Picture` (its route, page and subject; serialized onto the row; one variant today, every new kind is one more and every match learns it), `status_text` and `alt_text`; `HeraldCommand` and `parse_herald_command` for `/herald`; `HeraldState`, the session's handle and receiver. Reads no clock, does no I/O. |
| `mastodon.rs` | The Mastodon leg: `deliver` is the media upload (200, or 202 polled until ready) then the status call with the day's key as `Idempotency-Key`. Returns the status URL; logs nothing. |
| `bluesky.rs` | The Bluesky leg: `deliver` is the fit (`fit_under` shrinks the PNG under `BLOB_MAX_BYTES` with the `image` crate, on the blocking pool, before any call), a session from the app password, the blob upload, then `putRecord` under `tid_for(key)` with `facets` for the link and the tags. Returns the bsky.app URL; logs nothing. |

## Data

- `herald_posts` (model `late-core/src/models/herald.rs`, migrations 252 and 253): one row per UTC day, the claim. `id` is minted at the first claim and never changes: Mastodon sees it as the `Idempotency-Key`, Bluesky as the record key derived from it. `posting` is held while the calls run; `kind` (`artboard`; the check widens by forward migration per new kind), `subject` (the board's `daily:YYYY-MM-DD` key) and `picture` (the serialized `Picture`) are written at pick time, before the first call, so a retry delivers the same thing; `posted` means every configured network delivered; `skipped` is a day with nothing worth posting; `failed` keeps `attempts`. Claim semantics as `job_press_runs` and the paper's rows: a stale `posting` (older than `HERALD_STALE_CLAIM`, 20 minutes) is taken over, a `failed` row is claimed again under `HERALD_MAX_ATTEMPTS` (3), then the day rests.
- `herald_deliveries`: one row per network a day's picture landed on, written as each call succeeds; a retry sends only to the networks without one.
- `herald_settings` (`HeraldSwitch` in the same model): the operator's switch, one row keyed `posting`, read at every check, seeded off by migration 252. Herald-owned like `greendragon_settings`; the process-wide `app_flags` table was dropped in migration 219 and stays dropped.
- Reads of other domains, through model surfaces only: the board comes from `Snapshot::find_by_board_key` and is decoded with `dartboard_core::Canvas` to count ink.

## The post

- **When.** Every replica checks every `HERALD_CHECK_INTERVAL` (5 minutes). The day's post is due at `HERALD_POST_TIME`, 16:00 UTC (late afternoon in Europe, noon on the US east coast); `post_due_day` names the day owed, so a replica that was down at the hour catches up and stamps the right day. A settled day is memoized per replica, so the rest of the day costs one flag read per check and nothing else.
- **What.** Yesterday's daily Artboard snapshot, once it has `BOARD_MIN_INK` (200) drawn cells (the board is wiped on the first of the month, and the days after it are a few strokes). A bare or missing board is a `skipped` day, settled, not retried. The poster kind of the first design was dropped before launch: too few new posters to carry a slot. What comes next, and in what order, is in `AUDIENCE.md`.
- **Where the picture comes from.** late-web's PNG route (`late-web/CONTEXT.md`): `/gallery/{key}/image.png`, fetched once per attempt over `HeraldConfig::web_internal_url` (the cluster service in prod, the compose service in dev). One render a day on service-web's CPU.
- **The words.** One text for every network, under 300 graphemes (Bluesky's limit, the tightest; Mastodon allows 500): a line of plain speech, the page on late.sh, four tags (`#ASCIIart #MastoArt #tildeverse #ssh`). Nobody is named; when a kind credits a member it is `~name`, never `@name`, since a bare `@` is a mention of whoever has that name on the instance. The same alt text on every network.
- **Mastodon.** `POST /api/v2/media` as multipart (`file`, `description`), 200 with the attachment or 202 polled on `GET /api/v1/media/{id}` until 200; then `POST /api/v1/statuses` with `status`, `media_ids`, `visibility` (`MastodonConfig::visibility`: public in prod, unlisted in dev), `language: en` and the `Idempotency-Key` header. The status's `url` is the delivery's.
- **Bluesky.** `com.atproto.server.createSession` with the handle and the app password, `com.atproto.repo.uploadBlob` with the PNG (shrunk by 20% a side per pass until under `BLOB_MAX_BYTES`, 950,000, the PDS's cap being a megabyte), then `com.atproto.repo.putRecord` of an `app.bsky.feed.post` with the text, `langs`, the image embed (alt, blob, aspect ratio) and facets (`#link` for the page, `#tag` per hashtag, by UTF-8 byte range). The record key is a TID built from the day's UUID v7 (its millisecond timestamp and ten bits of its randomness), so the same day puts the same record. The delivery's URL is `https://bsky.app/profile/{handle}/post/{rkey}`. Bluesky has no unlisted post: a dev post there is public.
- **Never.** Chat, a frame of #lounge, anything a member did not make to be seen. Two posts a day on one network.

## Config and switches

- `Config::herald` (`HeraldConfig` in `late-ssh/src/config.rs`): `mastodon: Option<MastodonConfig>` (instance, token, visibility), `bluesky: Option<BlueskyConfig>` (pds, identifier, app password), `web_internal_url`. A network configured is the herald on (`enabled()`); none is off. Prod pins `https://fosstodon.org` and the Bluesky handle `late.sh` as literals and requires `LATE_HERALD_MASTODON_TOKEN` and `LATE_HERALD_BLUESKY_PASSWORD`, injected from the `herald-credentials` secret (`infra/secrets.tf`, `infra/service-ssh.tf`; GitHub secrets `HERALD_MASTODON_TOKEN` and `HERALD_BLUESKY_PASSWORD` through both terraform workflows). A new network is the same shape: pin its literal, `required` its secret, add the variable, the secret key, the env and the two workflow lines, shipped as an `-infra` release before the ssh image that needs it (`infra/RUNBOOK.md`, deploy skew rule). Dev: each network on only with its pair in `.env.local` (`LATE_HERALD_MASTODON_INSTANCE` + `..._TOKEN`, `LATE_HERALD_BLUESKY_IDENTIFIER` + `..._PASSWORD`; half a pair refuses to start), `web_internal_url` the compose web service, Mastodon unlisted. `Config::validate` refuses a configured network with an empty credential or a non-HTTP URL.
- The row: `/herald on` and `/herald off` in chat, admins only (the same statement through `scripts/connect_db.sh` works too); `off` stops the next check, no deploy. The config is the deployment's switch; the row is the operator's.
- `/herald status` reads the row and today's day (waiting, posting, posted where, skipped, failed after how many attempts). `/herald preview` shows what today's post would be and to which networks, sending nothing. `/herald post` works today's day now: it claims the row like the checker does, so a day goes out once however many admins type it, and the 16:00 check finds it settled. It ignores the row on purpose (an admin asking is the switch for that post) but not the config: with no network configured it says so. Every subcommand is refused with a banner for non-admins; the answer is one banner line (`svc::banner_for`), sent back over the service's channel to the session that asked.
- Account setup is by hand: Mastodon's bot flag and profile links in its web settings, the application with `write:media` and `write:statuses` under Development; Bluesky's app password under Settings, Privacy and security, and its `bot` self-label on the profile record (no setting in the app; any client that edits profile labels, or a one-off `putRecord` of `app.bsky.actor.profile` with `labels.values: [{val: "bot"}]`).

## Telemetry

- `late_ssh_herald_posts_total{result}`: `posted`, `skipped`, `lost` (another replica held the day; once per replica per day, then memoized), `failed`. `late_ssh_herald_deliveries_total{network,result}`: `delivered`, `failed` per leg. Grafana "Herald" row, `monitoring/dashboards/observability.json`.
- Spans `herald.check` around every check and `herald.command` around an admin's command. Error types `herald_flag_failed`, `herald_claim_failed`, `herald_delivery_failed` (per network, once, with the network), `herald_post_failed` (the day's summary), `herald_command_failed` (a command's read or flip failed); info lines `the herald delivered` (day, network, url), `the herald posted` (day, kind, subject, urls) and `the herald had nothing worth posting`; a warn when a failed day's row cannot be marked.

## Gotchas

- **Two idempotency mechanisms, one key.** Mastodon keeps an `Idempotency-Key` for about an hour; a status whose answer was lost is retried under the same key within 20 minutes (the stale bound) up to three times, inside the window. Bluesky's `putRecord` under the same TID is an upsert at any time. Either way a lost answer never double-posts; and a delivery that landed but could not be recorded is sent again under the same key, which both networks dedupe.
- **The picture is fetched per attempt, not per network**, and the pick is stored before the first call: a retry after a partial failure shows the same picture on the network that missed it.
- **The board is posted sight unseen.** Nothing looks at the picture between the midnight rollover and 16:00. The vandal ban and the day's own traffic usually clean a defaced board before midnight, but a slur drawn at 23:50 ships. The herald's copy on a network is removed by hand from the account. The check planned for member pieces (a vision model asked whether the picture is fit for a community art account, `AUDIENCE.md`) is the first guard; it is not built.
- **`/herald post` posts today, the checker posts the due day.** Before 16:00 the checker owes yesterday and the admin's command works today (with yesterday's board), so a hand post in the morning and the checker's catch-up of yesterday can both happen on one day; after 16:00 they are the same day and the row keeps it to one post.
- **The human half is operator work.** Answer replies, boost members, follow people (`AUDIENCE.md`). Fosstodon's rule for automated accounts is that an active member runs them; Bluesky's is that a bot never interacts unprompted, so boosting members there is done from a person's own account.

## Testing

- `late-core/src/models/herald_test.rs`: the claim (once per day, stale takeover, failed retry to the cap, the key surviving a retry), the stored pick and deliveries surviving a failure, the settle, the seeded switch flipping.
- `state_test.rs`: the due day, the words (under Bluesky's 300, no bare `@`, the page linked, four tags, the picture round-tripping through JSON), the look at the ink bar, the ink count, `/herald` parsing. `bluesky_test.rs`: the TID's shape, determinism and order, a big picture shrunk under the cap, the facets' byte ranges.
- `svc_test.rs`: fake late-web, Mastodon and Bluesky endpoints on one local port. Three days end to end on both networks (board, board, skipped), the row id as idempotency key and record key, the same text and alt on both, the picture fetched once, the switch off posting nothing, one network down leaving the day failed with the other's delivery kept and retried alone; the commands (the row flipping, status and preview reading without sending, `post` working today before 16:00 with the row off, the same day settled on a second `post`, the checker then leaving it).
- Run: `make test-llm ARGS="-p late-core -p late-ssh --lib -E 'test(herald)'"`.
